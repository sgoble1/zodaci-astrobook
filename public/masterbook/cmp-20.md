# Monthly Ritual Guide — July 2026 Through June 2027

*A sacred ritual practice, designed specifically for your chart, aligned
to the planetary rhythms of each month. Each month contains: a New Moon
intention, a Full Moon release, an altar suggestion, a body practice,
and a personal affirmation. These are not generic — they are written for
your chart's specific needs and activations in this specific year.*
