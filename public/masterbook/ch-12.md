The South Node represents the soul's accumulated past — the patterns, gifts, and tendencies that have been developed across previous lifetimes and that come naturally, almost automatically, in this one. Your South Node sits at 5°17' Leo in the seventh house of partnership, significant relationships, and the mirror of the other.

### Leo in the 7th House: The Devoted Performer

South Node Leo H7 describes a soul who has spent many lifetimes in the role of the devoted partner, the one who amplifies another's gifts while her own gifts waited in the wings. The Leo South Node in the house of partnership speaks of past lives as the performer, the one at the center of the stage, the one who loved with theatrical grandeur and received adoration in return — but who, in the process, may have lost track of her own authentic self in the service of being seen.

The South Node conjunct Jupiter in Leo H7 means that this past-life pattern was not merely comfortable — it was genuinely expansive and joyful. You were magnificent in that role. The soul's inheritance from those lifetimes is real: the warmth, the generosity, the capacity for theatrical love, the ability to make others feel seen and celebrated. These are gifts, not burdens. The work is not to abandon them but to redirect them.

### The Karmic Completion

The South Node in Leo H7 is being completed in this lifetime. The pattern of performing for love, of making yourself magnificent so that others will choose you, of centering your identity in the reflection you receive from significant others — this pattern is ready to be released. Not because it was wrong, but because it is finished. The soul has mastered it. The North Node in Aquarius H1 is calling you toward something new: the radical act of being yourself, for yourself, as the primary offering.
