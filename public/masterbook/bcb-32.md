Your Kua Number is 1, making you a member of the East Life Group in classical Feng Shui. Kua 1 is governed by the Water element, and its auspicious directions are North (Sheng Chi — growth, prosperity, career vitality), South (Tien Yi — health and healing), East (Nien Yen — love, harmony, and family relationships), and Southeast (Fu Wei — personal development and wisdom). Your inauspicious directions are Southwest, Northwest, West, and Northeast. Where possible, aligning your sleeping position, your working desk orientation, and your primary sitting and dining positions with one of your four auspicious directions will support the energetic quality of your daily life in ways that are cumulative and significant.

The Water element of your Kua 1 finds natural resonance with your Part of Fortune in Cancer — both pointing toward the nourishing, flowing, emotionally attuned quality of Water as your elemental home. Water element décor includes colors in the blue-black spectrum, flowing and curved shapes, mirrors (which in Feng Shui carry Water’s reflective quality), and actual water features such as fountains, aquariums, or bowls of clean, still water in the North sector of your home. Your home’s North sector is the career and life path sector in the Bagua system, and for a Kua 1 native this is also your most powerful growth direction. Activating the North sector of your home — with a water feature, with clean, organized energy, with images or objects that speak to your professional aspirations and your life’s mission — is one of the most aligned Feng Shui actions you can take for your particular chart.

### Room-by-Room Recommendations

Your entrance and foyer should convey the expansive, global, philosophical quality of your Sagittarius Ascendant: high ceilings if architecturally possible, or the visual illusion of height created through tall mirrors or artwork; artwork from places you have traveled or places that represent the wider world you are oriented toward; natural light, or warm lighting that mimics it; and at least one object that immediately signals to anyone entering that this is a home where ideas and beauty and genuine character are present. The entrance is the face the home turns to the world, and your face is Sagittarian — make it feel like a doorway to something larger than the ordinary.

Your bedroom should be oriented for sleeping with your head pointing North (Sheng Chi) or East (Nien Yen) for optimal Kua 1 alignment. The bedroom serves your Chiron in Cancer in the 7th house and your Part of Fortune in Cancer: it should feel emotionally safe, genuinely nurturing, and profoundly restful. Soft, flowing fabrics; colors in the blue-green-white spectrum of Cancer’s water and lunar associations; minimal electronic stimulation; and a quality of gentle beauty rather than dramatic statement are what your bedroom needs. This is the room where you let down the Capricorn stellium’s armor and the Aries Moon’s fire. Make it worth coming home to.

Your office or workspace should feel powerful, private, and intensely purposeful — a command center for your Mercury Final Dispositor and your Pluto H10 professional mission. Your desk should face North or East for your Kua number. The workspace should be organized in a way that reflects your analytical mind: clear surfaces where active projects are worked, organized archives where completed or referenced material is stored, and an absence of the visual clutter that overstimulates your Virgo nervous system. Include one object of genuine beauty — a crystal, a meaningful art piece, a plant — to honor your Libra MC’s need for elegance even in the work environment. And ensure that your workspace receives or can replicate good natural light, because your Mercury Rx in the 8th house already works in the dark enough; give it the contrast of physical brightness when it surfaces to write.

Your living room — your primary space for communication, connection, and the expression of your Moon Aries in the 3rd house — should be energizing, warm, and genuinely stimulating. Books are essential and should be beautiful and accessible, not merely decorative. The Sagittarius Ascendant home is a home full of ideas made visible: maps, globes, artwork from different cultural traditions, philosophical quotes, objects that have stories attached to them. The living room should invite conversation that goes somewhere real. Furniture arranged for genuine dialogue rather than passive entertainment. A space where the Moon handle’s need to express, exchange, and communicate can be met by the physical environment itself. The Southeast corner of your living room corresponds to Fu Wei — personal development — and placing a plant, a crystal, or meaningful learning materials here activates this energy center in harmony with your chart’s North Node Aquarius direction of authentic self-development.
