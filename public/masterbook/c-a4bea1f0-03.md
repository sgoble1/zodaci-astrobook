PART THREE: Venus Retrograde

Personal Guide

Venus turns retrograde approximately every eighteen months for a period of about forty days, tracing a backward arc through the sky that astrologers have observed for thousands of years as a period of relationship review, value recalibration, and the resurfacing of unresolved emotional material from past loves. Venus Retrograde is especially significant for April because of where

Venus sits in her natal chart: 29°20' Leo in the eighth house, at the anaretic degree — the final, crisis-point degree of the sign, carrying the energy of completion, urgency, and earned wisdom. Love, for April, has never been casual.

It has always carried the weight of the eighth house — depth, transformation, intimacy that goes all the way down, the kind of love that changes who you are at your foundation. The anaretic Venus says that what she came into this life to learn about love is at the very edge of being fully understood. Each Venus

Retrograde is one more opportunity to complete the learning — to look at the history of how she has loved, what she has given, what she has tolerated, and what she has withheld, and to bring the pattern into full consciousness so that it can be transformed.
