Section 26: Enneagram — Type 4w3,

The Aristocrat The Enneagram is a nine-point personality framework rooted in ancient wisdom traditions and refined through twentieth-century psychological scholarship.

Unlike personality assessments that describe behavior, the Enneagram describes the core motivational structure beneath behavior — the fundamental emotional wound, the core fear, the core desire, and the characteristic coping strategy that each type deploys in response to the wound. For April, the Enneagram profile consistent with her natal chart configuration, her personal history, and the specific quality of her life experience is Type 4 with a 3 Wing (4w3) — sometimes called the Aristocrat or the Elite. This is not a flattering title in the conventional sense; it describes a person who has an innate sense of being meant for something of quality and significance, and who experiences deep pain when the circumstances of her life appear to contradict that sense.
