### The 7th House: Blessings Through Partnership

In the seventh house, all of this generosity and expansion is oriented toward partnership: toward the significant others, the collaborators, the mirror relationships that reflect back what you cannot always see in yourself. This placement means that your greatest blessings arrive through people. Your most significant growth happens in relationship. Your luck, when it flows, tends to flow through a partner, a collaborator, a mentor, or someone who believes in you with the particular warmth and dramatic generosity that Leo Jupiter in H7 describes.
