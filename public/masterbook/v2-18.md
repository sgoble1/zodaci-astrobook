# Year Ahead Forecast

July 2026 Through June 2027

The forecast that follows is built on the confirmed transit positions of
June 29, 2026 and projects forward through June 2027. Each month is read
through the lens of the natal chart — the transits are understood in
relationship to the specific placements they are activating, not as
abstract planetary movements. This is your chart's story for the next
twelve months. Read each month in full when you arrive at it. Return to
what was written for the month before and note what proved accurate. The
chart is a living document; the forecast is its next chapter in real
time.

July 2026 — Saturn Exact on the Moon; Jupiter Return Imminent

**Key Transit:** Saturn in Aries conjunct natal Moon H3 (bucket handle —
at maximum pressure); Jupiter approaching natal Jupiter at 3°59' Leo for
the Return exact.

July 2026 is the month of maximum pressure on the bucket handle and the
final approach to the Jupiter Return exact. Saturn exact on the Moon H3
means that the chart's single most sensitive point — the handle of the
entire bucket configuration — is under the full weight of Saturn's
structural demand. This is not comfortable. Saturn on the Moon asks:
what have you been feeling that you have not yet said? What has been
building in the H3 communication channel that needs to be spoken or
written? The pressure is not to silence the Moon. It is to give the
Moon's directness a structure — to require that the immediate emotional
expressiveness of the Aries Moon be given the weight and precision of
Saturn before it releases. What is said in July should be said carefully
and completely. Not hesitantly — but deliberately. Meanwhile, Jupiter is
approaching its exact natal degree at 3°59' Leo — the chart ruler is
almost home. Partnerships, opportunities, and expansions that arrived
with the June 30 entry into Leo are now deepening. By the end of July,
the Jupiter Return exact is either imminent or occurring. What arrives
in this window is Jupiter-Return quality. Receive it as such. **Action
Step:** Begin writing practice this month — write for twenty minutes
daily, without editing. Let the Saturn-Moon pressure produce its
document. The writing done in July 2026 may be the most important
writing of this decade.
