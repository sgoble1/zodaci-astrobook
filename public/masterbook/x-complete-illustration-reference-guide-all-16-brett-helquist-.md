The following complete illustration reference guide provides full art direction, technical specifications, and AI generation prompts for all sixteen pen-and-ink illustrations commissioned for this book. All illustrations are designed in the style of Brett Helquist — the illustrator of A Series of Unfortunate Events — characterized by dense Victorian crosshatching, dramatic chiaroscuro, confident contour lines, and selective Pantone 871 metallic gold accents. The global style guide applies to all sixteen illustrations.
