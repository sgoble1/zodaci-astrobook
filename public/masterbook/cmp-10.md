returns to its exact natal degree of Virgo 13°56', provides a detailed
forecast for the year beginning September 6, 2026. This is the chart of
your 36th year — and given the extraordinary convergence of 2026 timing
factors, this Solar Return is one of the most significant of your entire
life.*

of this Solar Return year:

  natal H7, activating the SR 7th house of partnership and bringing the
