Eclipses are among the most powerful timing triggers in astrology. They occur when the Sun and Moon align at or near the lunar nodes — the North and South Nodes of the Moon — creating either a Solar Eclipse (New Moon at the nodes) or a Lunar Eclipse (Full Moon at the nodes). Eclipses that fall near your natal planets or angles activate those placements with extraordinary intensity, often triggering events or realizations that have been building for months or years.
