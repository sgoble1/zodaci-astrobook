birthday. The Solar Return is not a transit chart and not a natal chart

for that particular solar year. The Solar Return Ascendant (which
changes each year depending on your location at the time of the return)

the SR chart describe the energies most available and most demanding of
attention. Every Solar Return chart exists in conversation with the
natal: the SR Venus in a particular house tells you where the natal
Venus themes will be most expressed that year, filtered through the SR
sign. The September 6, 2026 Solar Return — the birthday that opens the
36th year and the First House profection year — is one of the most
significant Solar Returns of April's life to date.
