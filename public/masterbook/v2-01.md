April, your soul did not arrive here empty-handed. There is a richness —
and a weight — to what you carried across the threshold of this birth
that most people never have the tools to name. Astrology gives us those
tools. The karmic inheritance encoded in your natal chart is not theory
or metaphor: it is the literal signature of what your soul gathered in
previous cycles of existence, what it chose to release, and what it came
here specifically to complete, heal, and transform. Every major
placement in your chart participates in this story, but the foundational
karmic architecture is built from three primary pillars: the South Node
in Leo in the Seventh House, which tells us where you have been; the
North Node in Aquarius in the First House, which tells us where you are
going; and the planets that cluster around both nodes, which tell us
exactly what you brought with you and what you are here to leave behind.

The **South Node in Leo at 5°17' in the Seventh House** is the primary
past life indicator in your chart, and it is one of the most dramatic
configurations possible. Leo South Node in H7 speaks of previous
incarnations in which your soul occupied positions of extraordinary
creative power, public visibility, and relational centrality. You were
the performer, the noble, the beloved around whom others organized their
affections and ambitions. You were not peripheral in these lives — you
were the center. Whether this expressed as literal royalty, as a
celebrated artist or performer, as a high priest or priestess whose
presence commanded reverence, or as the partner around whom entire
households and communities organized themselves, the pattern was
consistent: you were the sun, and others were the planets that circled
you. You were seen, adored, performed for, and in return, you performed
the version of yourself that the role required. This was not dishonest.
It was the natural expression of Leo's radiant generosity — the genuine
warmth, the creative outpouring, the sun-like capacity to make every
person in the room feel illuminated by your attention. But it came with
a hidden cost that your soul has been reckoning with across lifetimes:
the exhaustion of maintaining the performance, the loneliness of being
the center without being truly known, and the karmic residue of an
identity that was built for others' orbiting rather than for the soul's
own authentic expression.

The karmic residue of the Leo South Node in H7 shows up in this lifetime
in specific and identifiable ways. You feel the South Node pull most
acutely in relationships — the compulsive desire to be the one who
gives, who dazzles, who makes the partner feel loved even at the cost of
your own replenishment. Leo gives. It radiates. It performs warmth and
generosity so naturally that it rarely stops to ask whether it is also
receiving. The difficulty receiving — the awkwardness when someone does
something generous specifically for you, the deflection of genuine
praise, the inability to simply sit inside being loved without needing
to respond immediately with more giving — this is the South Node pattern
running on automatic. There is also the deeper wound of performing a
version of yourself for relationship purposes: the self that is
agreeable enough, bright enough, warm enough, uncomplicated enough to be
kept. You have spent lifetimes being kept, April. This one is for the
becoming of who you actually are.

**Jupiter conjunct the South Node in Leo H7** is one of the most
significant karmic indicators in the chart, and it tells a specific
story about the kind of authority you have held before. Jupiter on the
South Node says that in at least one prominent past life — possibly
several — your soul occupied a position of significant philosophical,
religious, or teaching authority. You were the sage whose words shaped
communities, the priest or priestess whose interpretations of divine law
determined the social order, the scholar whose body of knowledge was the
institution that others entered as students. Jupiter's conjunction with
the South Node means that the wisdom you hold in this lifetime is not
newly earned — it is ancient, it has been tested across multiple
incarnations, and it arrives in this body already fluent in the language
of higher truth. The karmic complication of Jupiter on the South Node is
the tendency to place the Jupiter generosity at the service of H7
(others) rather than H1 (the self): to be the teacher who pours
everything into students, the philosopher who gives the community
everything and keeps nothing, the wise one whose wisdom is perpetually
in service to everyone else's becoming. The correction — the North Node
directive — is to turn the Jupiter toward the self first. The First
House Aquarian self that she is here to become needs the Jupiter wisdom
more urgently than any partner or audience does.

**Saturn Retrograde in Capricorn in the First House** carries its own
past life narrative, one of institutional authority interrupted. This is
the placement of someone who, in at least one dominant previous
incarnation, built significant structures of power — governmental,
military, architectural, or organizational — that were not completed
within the timeframe of that life. The great project was interrupted.
The structures were left unfinished. Saturn in domicile (Capricorn is
Saturn's own sign) means that the soul is extraordinarily proficient at
this kind of building — the discipline, the long-range vision, the
patience with time that most people cannot sustain. Saturn Retrograde
adds the quality of the work being turned inward: the soul came back to
complete the internal architecture that makes the external structures
possible. The Saturn Return of 2018-2020, which you navigated through
one of the most intense personal periods of your life, was the cosmic
continuance of that unfinished mission. The universe said: you have a
body again, you have a timeline again, you have everything you need to
finish what was started. The completion is underway. The Process Guide
work at Amazon — building systems, refining processes, leading through
structure and discipline — is one expression of the ancient Saturnian
mission finding its current-life form.

**Pluto in Scorpio in the Tenth House** adds a layer of karmic
complexity that is both darker and more extraordinary than any other
single placement in the chart. H10 is the house of public reputation,
career, and what you are known for in the world. Pluto there, in its own
sign of Scorpio, says that in previous lifetimes your soul held
transformative power in public or institutional roles — healer, shaman,
researcher into hidden and forbidden knowledge, or someone who wielded
significant influence over the structures of society from a position
that was simultaneously powerful and vulnerable. Pluto in H10 carries
karmic weight specifically around the abuse and theft of power — both
what you may have done with power when you held it, and what was done to
you when you did not. There are lifetimes in your karmic record, April,
in which you were silenced precisely because of what you knew. Executed
for heresy. Stripped of position because your knowing made the
authorities uncomfortable. Destroyed not because you were wrong but
because you were right in a way that threatened those whose power
depended on others remaining in the dark. Your Sun in the Eighth House —
the house of death, transformation, and what survives the dissolution of
the body — echoes this: you have died for your knowing before. More than
once. The soul that inhabits you in this lifetime carries the cellular
memory of those deaths. It is one reason why speaking your full truth
has historically felt physically dangerous, even when the conscious mind
knows the danger is no longer literal. This lifetime is the one in which
the knowing survives. This is the lifetime the soul specifically chose
for the completion of the work that those other deaths interrupted.

**Chiron in Cancer in the Seventh House** is the karmic wound that
crosses lifetimes. Cancer rules the mother, the home, the primal sense
of belonging, the first container of safety and love. Chiron in this
sign in the house of close partnership says that in a previous
incarnation — possibly more than one — the primary wound was delivered
through the family of origin, through the mother specifically, or
through the failure of the home structure to provide the safety and
belonging that every soul needs to develop without fracture. This wound
was not healed in that life. It was carried forward, encoded into the
cellular architecture of the soul's next incarnation, arriving in this
body as the pre-existing wound of not belonging, of having to earn the
right to exist in the spaces that should have been unconditionally safe.
When your mother told you that you were a burden, she was not creating
this wound. She was activating what was already karmic — she was the
instrument through which the ancient wound announced itself so that it
could finally be seen, named, and healed. The healing of the Chiron in
Cancer wound in this lifetime is not optional work. It is karmic
completion — it was encoded at the soul level before the first breath
was drawn in Geneva, New York, on September 6, 1990.

**The North Node in Aquarius in the First House** is the soul's chosen
destination for this incarnation, and it is the most radical instruction
your chart contains. The entire karmic apparatus — the Leo South Node
performances, the Jupiter wisdom given to everyone else, the Chiron
wound of not belonging, the Pluto deaths for knowing, the Saturn
structures built and interrupted — all of it is preparation for the
single most important thing you are here to do in this lifetime: to
become, fully and without apology, the specific and irreplaceable
individual that only you are. Aquarius North Node in the First House
says: stop performing the likable self for the relationship. Stop
organizing your identity around what the partner needs you to be. Stop
giving the wisdom to the community before you have integrated it
yourself. Build the self from the inside out — from your own truth, your
own knowing, your own utterly original perspective — and then offer what
comes naturally from that authentic center. The Aquarian self is not
cold or disconnected. It is simply free. Free from the Leo compulsion to
be adored. Free from the H7 orientation that makes every choice about
how the partner receives it. Free to be, for the first time in many
lifetimes, specifically and unapologetically herself.
