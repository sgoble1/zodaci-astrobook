### Print Production Specifications

> • Interior paper: cream-toned archival quality, ideally 100gsm uncoated; avoid bright white.
>
> • Cover/final full-page art: heavier stock, 200gsm minimum or mounted cover boards for case-bound construction.
>
> • Binding: case-bound with sewn signatures preferred so half/full-page art opens flat without gutter loss.
>
> • Gold: Pantone 871 metallic spot ink recommended; CMYK approximation only as fallback (C:0 M:18 Y:55 K:25).
>
> • Black: rich black for large dark coverage; 100K black for fine linework where appropriate.
>
> • Proofing: verify gold plates, small line retention, title clearances, spine width, and edge bleed before press.

### Final Art QA Checklist

> • Every illustration has one dominant specified light source and maintains high-contrast crosshatching.
>
> • April's posture remains composed, forward-capable, and never defeated.
>
> • Gold appears only on sacred/significant objects: Galactic Center, candles, labels, glyphs, crown, dawn, selected borders.
>
> • All dimensions and document placements match the placement map.
>
> • Illustrations 01 and 16 form a deliberate pair: dark threshold vs dawn forward motion; final image reverses shadows behind her.
>
> • No AI output should be treated as final art unless redrawn/refined to match deliberate pen-and-ink linework.
