The Arabic Parts (or Lots) are calculated points that combine three chart factors — the Ascendant, Sun, Moon, or other planets — to reveal hidden dimensions of the chart. For a Day Chart like April's, the formulas shift slightly from the Night Chart formulas. The Arabic Parts are among the oldest techniques in the astrological tradition — they were used by the ancient Babylonians, the ancient Egyptians, the ancient Greeks, and the medieval Arabic astrologers who gave them their name. They reveal dimensions of the chart that are not visible in the ordinary aspect structure — the hidden connections, the secret relationships, the invisible threads that bind the chart's elements together.
