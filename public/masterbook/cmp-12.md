# Chakra Alignment & Planetary Correspondences

*Each chakra center corresponds to specific planetary energies and natal
placements in your chart. Understanding where each energy center is
blocked, flowing, or awaiting activation allows you to work with your
chart on the level of the body — which, for a chart with Saturn Rx H1,
Neptune Rx H1, and Chiron Cancer H7, is where much of the most important
work lives.*
