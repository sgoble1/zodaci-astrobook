# Monthly Ritual Guide

A Year of Sacred Practice — July 2026 Through June 2027

This ritual guide is built on the architecture of your specific chart
and your specific transits. Every ritual below is designed to honor the
Aries Moon bucket handle — which is most powerful in ritual at the Full
Moon, when the opposition between the natal Sun and the natal Moon is
most activated — and the Saturn H1 structure, which responds to
consistent, recurring practice more than to sporadic inspiration. These
rituals are not meant to be elaborate or difficult. They are meant to be
real. Astrology honors sincerity over spectacle. Your Saturn H1
recognizes genuine, consistent, structured commitment. Your Aries Moon
bucket handle responds to action taken first and analysis done second.
Begin with what you have. Bring what you feel. Let the chart do the
rest.

<td><p>July 2026 — Saturn Exact on the Moon: The Ritual of the Weighted
Word</p>
<p><strong>When:</strong> The Full Moon of July 2026 (approximately July
3-4, in Capricorn — activating your H1 stellium against the Moon's H3
handle).</p>
<p><strong>Materials:</strong> A notebook, a pen that writes with
weight, a single candle, a piece of paper you can burn or bury
afterward.</p>
<p><strong>Ritual:</strong> Light the candle. Write, in full sentences,
every significant thing you have not yet said — the truths that are
fully formed inside and have not yet been spoken. Write until the list
is complete. Then read each item aloud, by candlelight, in your own
voice. Saturn on the Moon asks that the words exist in sound, not just
in ink. After reading each aloud, mark which ones are ready to be said
to their actual intended recipient in the coming month. The paper is
your commitment. The candle is the witness. The speaking aloud is the
opening of the bucket handle under Saturn's structure. Put the notebook
somewhere you will return to in September and note what
changed.</p></td>

marked to unmarked is the current state of the North Node arrival. For
each unmarked belief, write one piece of counter-evidence from your
actual life. Begin releasing the unmarked beliefs through the Mercury Rx
period — not in a single dramatic moment, but through the gradual,
considered, Virgo-precise process of replacing each inherited belief
with the evidence-based truth.</p></td>
