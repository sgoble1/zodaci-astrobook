Section 28: Astrocartography — Your

Power Lines on the World Map Astrocartography — also called Locational Astrology — is the practice of projecting the natal chart onto the surface of the Earth to identify which geographic lines activate which planetary energies for a specific individual. Developed by Jim Lewis in the 1970s and subsequently refined by numerous practitioners, it maps four types of lines for each planet: the ASC line (where a planet was rising on the eastern horizon at birth), the DSC line (where a planet was setting on the western horizon), the MC line (where a planet was at its highest point, the Midheaven), and the IC line (where a planet was at its lowest point, the nadir). Each line creates a geographic corridor, roughly 300 miles wide, where the energy of that planet becomes dramatically amplified in the person's lived experience. Living on, working on, or traveling to these lines produces marked effects in the life domains associated with the planet in question.
