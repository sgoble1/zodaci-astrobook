# Chapter 37 — Major Transits 2026–2028: The Cosmic Weather Ahead

The transit picture for the years 2026 through 2028 is, by any astrological measure, one of the most significant and concentrated periods of your life. Multiple major outer planet transits are converging simultaneously, each addressing a different dimension of your chart and calling a different part of your nature into active transformation and growth. Understanding each transit individually — and then seeing how they operate in concert — gives you the fullest possible picture of what these years are asking of you and what extraordinary possibilities they contain.

### Saturn in Aries (2025–2028): Identity Restructured

Saturn’s transit through Aries from approximately March 2025 through mid-2028 creates a square to your natal Capricorn stellium in the 1st house: Saturn in Aries squaring Saturn in Capricorn, squaring Uranus in Capricorn, and squaring Neptune in Capricorn. This is the second Saturn square of your life — the one that arrives in the mid-to-late thirties and asks you to evaluate, revise, and refine the structures you have been building since your first Saturn return. The square from Aries is demanding: Aries pushes Saturn’s disciplined authority into a test of genuine courage, asking whether the structures you have constructed for your life are built on authentic initiative or on the accommodation of others’ expectations. The square to Uranus in Capricorn asks whether the revolutionary dimension of your identity — the Uranian freedom-seeker in the 1st house — has been given adequate room, or whether it has been managed and contained past the point of productive discipline into the territory of suppression. The square to Neptune in Capricorn asks about spiritual integrity: whether the mystical sensitivity in your 1st house has been honored in the structures of your daily life or whether it has been sacrificed to the demands of practical performance. Each of these squares will feel, at different points in the transit’s passage, like pressure — like the walls of your current life are too small for what you are becoming. That feeling is the transit working. The walls ARE too small. Saturn in Aries is inviting you to rebuild them to a more genuine size. Navigate this transit by taking courageous initiative in the specific areas where you have been waiting for permission that was never going to come from outside. The permission is internal. Saturn in Aries square your Capricorn stellium is the transit that forces that recognition.

### Uranus in Gemini (2025–2033): The Creative Revolution

Uranus’s move into Gemini in 2025 is the beginning of an eight-year period of revolutionary change in all Gemini-ruled domains of collective life: communication, information, ideas, technology, transportation, and the exchange of knowledge at every level. For you personally, this transit has a specific and extraordinary impact: Uranus in Gemini will form a conjunction with your natal Mars in Gemini at 3 degrees in the 5th house of creative expression. Uranus conjunct natal Mars is one of the most dramatically energizing transits possible — an electrification of the planet of drive and desire by the planet of revolution and sudden awakening. In the 5th house of creative expression, this conjunction describes a period of explosive creative energy: the arrival of entirely new creative impulses, the sudden availability of creative opportunities that were not previously imaginable, the disruption of whatever creative patterns have become habitual in favor of something more genuinely alive and more authentically yours. If you have been a writer who has not been writing, or a teacher who has not been teaching, or a creator of any kind whose creative expression has been in a period of quieter preparation — Uranus conjunct your Mars in the 5th house is the transit that ends the preparation period and begins the expression period. It may feel sudden. It will almost certainly feel electric. Follow it. Even when — especially when — it leads you somewhere you did not plan to go.

### Neptune in Aries (2025–2039): The Foundation Dissolves

Neptune’s long transit through Aries will, over the course of many years, approach your natal Moon in Aries at 6 degrees in the 3rd house. Neptune conjuncting the natal Moon is one of the most spiritually potent and emotionally challenging transits in the astrological repertoire: it dissolves the boundaries around the emotional self, opening the Moon’s perceptive range to extraordinary breadth while simultaneously making it more difficult to maintain the clear emotional boundaries that ordinary functioning requires. In your 3rd house of communication, this transit has particular implications for your writing and expressive life: Neptune conjuncting your Moon handle may bring a period of extraordinary creative and intuitive writing, of language that comes from a place more fluid and more mystically sourced than your Mercury Rx’s analytical precision typically allows. It may also bring periods of emotional confusion or heightened sensitivity to your immediate environment. The practical guidance for this transit: protect your writing time, take seriously whatever comes to you in the hypnagogic state between waking and sleep, and be gentle with your nervous system during periods of intense emotional sensitivity. Neptune is not visiting your Moon to damage it; it is visiting to expand what your emotional intelligence can perceive and ultimately express.

### Pluto in Aquarius (2024–2044): Destiny Powered

The transit of Pluto through Aquarius over the next two decades is the single most significant long-range transit of your life, and it is active now. As Pluto moves through Aquarius in the coming years, it will approach your natal North Node at 5 degrees and 17 minutes of Aquarius — a transit of Pluto conjunct natal North Node that is so rare that most people do not experience it in a single lifetime. The last time Pluto was in Aquarius was from 1777 to 1798 — the era of the American and French Revolutions, of the complete dismantling of the old political order and the construction of the new. This is the energy that is now moving through your North Node: the total transformation of your soul’s intended destiny point by the planet of absolute, irrevocable, death-and-rebirth change. What this means for you is that your North Node mission — the Aquarian revolutionary who shows up authentically in service of the collective — is being powered by the most transformative force in the solar system. This is not a gentle nudge toward your destiny. This is a complete and irrevocable calling. The work you are being called to do in the world, the authentic self you are being called to become, the contribution you are being called to make — Pluto will power all of it, and it will not allow you to remain in the comfortable safety of the South Node Leo pattern of partnership-defined identity. Your destiny is being activated by the most powerful force in the chart. Rise to meet it.

### Jupiter Through Cancer and Leo (2025–2027): Abundance and Expansion

Jupiter’s transit through Cancer in 2025–2026 activates your Part of Fortune in Cancer and your Chiron in Cancer, both in the 7th house. This is a period of genuine healing and abundance in the relational domain: Jupiter expanding and blessing your Part of Fortune brings a quality of joy and flourishing to your closest relationships that may feel unprecedented in its generosity. Jupiter expanding and blessing your Chiron Cancer is a period of significant relational healing — of the wound being met with genuine grace, of old patterns around receiving care being softened and resolved, of encounters in partnership that teach you at last what it feels like to be truly held. In 2026–2027, Jupiter moves into Leo, directly conjuncting your natal Jupiter in Leo in the 7th house, your Venus in Leo, and the Vertex. This is your Jupiter return — a twelve-year completion cycle — and it is occurring simultaneously with the Leo Venus and Vertex activation. The Jupiter return in Leo in the 7th house is one of the most expansive and joyful transits your chart will experience in this lifetime: a period of genuine abundance in love, creative expression, and partnership, of the universe offering the warmth and celebration and soul-level recognition that your Venus at the anaretic degree has been reaching toward across the whole of this life. Receive it. You have earned it.
