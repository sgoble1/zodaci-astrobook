*The Complete Architecture*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

The twelve houses of the natal chart describe the twelve domains of life — the arenas in which the planetary energies play out. Each house has a natural sign, a natural ruler, and a set of life themes it governs. What follows is a complete analysis of all twelve houses in your chart, including the planets that occupy them, the signs on their cusps, and the specific way each domain of life is colored by your unique configuration.

You carry an entire civilization inside your body. |
