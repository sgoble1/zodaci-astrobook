Feng Shui — literally 'wind and water' in Chinese — is the ancient art of arranging the physical environment to support the flow of life energy (chi) in alignment with the individual's specific energetic profile. It is one of the oldest and most sophisticated systems of environmental design in the world, with roots in ancient Chinese cosmology, astronomy, and the observation of natural patterns. The fundamental premise of Feng Shui is that the physical environment is not neutral — it is alive with energy, and the arrangement of that energy either supports or undermines the individual's health, relationships, career, and spiritual development.
