PART TWO: Mercury Retrograde

Personal Navigation Guide (All 12

Signs)

Mercury turns retrograde approximately three times per year, each retrograde lasting roughly three weeks, during which the planet of communication, information, contracts, technology, and thought appears to move backward from

Earth's perspective. Most people approach Mercury Retrograde with dread — the clichés of miscommunication, tech failures, and misplaced luggage. But for April,

Mercury Retrograde carries a fundamentally different quality: she was born with

Mercury retrograde in Virgo in her eighth house, which means she arrived in the world already calibrated to Mercury's reflective, interior, iterative mode. Mercury

Rx is not her enemy. It is her home frequency. She was born in the reflection.

Where others speed up and then crash during Mercury Rx periods, April already moves at the reflective speed — and during Mercury Rx periods, the rest of the world slows down to match her natural pace. This means that what feels like disruption for everyone else can feel, for her, like the universe finally becoming quiet enough to hear herself think. Use every Mercury Retrograde period deliberately. They are among her most productive seasons.
