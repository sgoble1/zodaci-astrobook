# Annual Profections

Your Age-35 Time Lord & Annual Theme

Annual profections are one of the most elegant timing techniques in
Hellenistic astrology — a system developed by the ancient Greeks and
refined over centuries that activates one house of the natal chart per
year of life, cycling through all twelve houses in order over twelve
years, and then beginning again. The process is simple and powerful: at
age zero, the First House is active. At age one, the Second House
activates. At age twelve, the cycle returns to the First House. The
planet that rules the sign on the cusp of the activated house becomes
the Time Lord — the planetary governor of that entire year of life. This
Time Lord is then examined in both the natal chart and by current
transit to understand what the year is designed to produce. Profections
do not describe every event of the year — they describe the year's
central theme, its organizing lesson, and the specific planetary energy
most available to you as a resource and most demanding of your
attention. They are, in practice, one of the most reliable timing tools
in the entire astrological toolkit.

Age 35 — The Twelfth House Profection Year (September 6, 2025 —
September 5, 2026)

At age 35, the annual profection activates your Twelfth House. In your
chart, the Twelfth House cusp falls in Scorpio, making **Pluto** the
Time Lord for this entire year. This combination is among the most
powerful and transformative profection years possible — and it arrives
at exactly the right moment. The Twelfth House is simultaneously the
most private and the most spiritually expansive house in the chart. It
rules the unconscious, the spaces that are neither fully inner nor fully
outer — retreats, large institutions, places of solitude and reflection.
It rules what is hidden, what is given away unknowingly, what lies
beneath the surface of the conscious personality. It also rules large
institutional environments — hospitals, prisons, monasteries, and, in
the modern context, large corporate workplaces like Amazon Fulfillment
Centers. Pluto as Time Lord of this house in this year is not gentle.
Pluto excavates. It brings to the surface what has been buried in the
Twelfth House's deep unconscious — the old grief, the old anger, the old
programming about worth and belonging — and transforms it. The
transformation is not comfortable, but it is permanent: what Pluto
transforms does not revert. This year — which you are in the final
months of right now as of June 2026 — is the year that clears the
ground. It is the year that processes what needs to be processed so that
the First House year that begins on September 6, 2026 can be built on
cleared, honest, transformed ground rather than on the old foundation's
unexamined rubble.

Age 36 — The First House Profection Year (September 6, 2026 — September
5, 2027)

Beginning on your 36th birthday, the profection wheel returns to the
First House — the house of identity, body, self-presentation, and the
face you show the world. In your chart, the First House is ruled by
Sagittarius, making **Jupiter** the Time Lord for age 36. This is your
chart ruler becoming the Time Lord of your identity house — a moment of
extraordinary self-authorization. The First House profection year is the
opening of a complete new 12-year chapter. Everything you have processed
and transformed in the 12th house year of age 35 becomes the foundation
for who you introduce yourself as in this new cycle. You do not arrive
at 36 as the same person who began the previous cycle at 24. You arrive
as someone who has been through the Saturn Return, who has survived
grief, who has done the 12th house depth work, who has had her Jupiter
Return open the door to entirely new partnerships and possibilities.
Jupiter as Time Lord in a First House profection year says: this is the
year that your authentic self becomes more visible, more expansive, more
available to you than it has ever been. This is the year that identity
becomes something you claim rather than something that is assigned to
you. The entire current window — the Jupiter Return in Leo H7, the Pluto
approaching the North Node, the progressed Ascendant meeting the North
Node — all of it converges on this birthday as the most significant
beginning of your adult life.
