Monthly Strategies

As a Full Moon birth, April's monthly cycle is more viscerally experienced than most people's. The Full Moon — which occurs approximately once a month when the Sun and Moon reach their maximum opposition — is the return of her natal condition: that luminous, high-contrast moment when both lights are simultaneously at their fullest. Full Moon periods will always feel like activation for April. She will be more emotionally present, more communicative, more creatively lit, more sensitive, more aware of everything in the room. This is not instability. This is the chart coming home to its birth condition once a month.

The task during Full Moon periods is not to manage or suppress this intensity but to direct it — through the handle (expression, communication, creative output), toward the direction the rest of the chart is building.

New Moon periods are the opposite experience: the sky is dark, the Sun and

Moon are together (in the same sign, on the same side of the zodiac), and the chart's fundamental tension is temporarily released. For a Full Moon birth, New

Moon periods can feel strangely quiet — even flat. There may be a sense of reduced energy, reduced emotional access, reduced inspiration. This is not depression; it is the chart's rest cycle. The New Moon is when intentions are planted, not when they flower. The New Moon in Virgo each September — occurring near April's birthday — is the single most important monthly lunation of the entire year: the Sun's return (Solar Return) and the Moon's monthly reset
