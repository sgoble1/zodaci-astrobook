# Chapter 15 — Decans & Dwads: The Sub-Signatures of Your Placements

Every sign of the zodiac is divided into three decans of ten degrees each, and each decan has a planetary sub-ruler that adds a layer of nuance to any planet or point that falls within it. Beyond the decan, each sign can be further divided into twelve dwads of two and a half degrees each, with each dwad corresponding to one of the twelve signs. These sub-signatures are rarely discussed in introductory astrology, but they carry genuine interpretive weight — they are the fine print of the natal chart, the overtone that colors the fundamental note of each placement.

### Your Sun: Second Decan of Virgo (10°00′–19°59′) — Saturn Sub-Ruler

Your Sun at 13 degrees and 56 minutes of Virgo falls in the second decan, which is sub-ruled by Saturn in the Chaldean decan system. This adds a distinctly Saturnian coloring to your already analytical Virgo Sun: a gravity, a seriousness, a tendency toward the long view and the structured approach. The Saturn sub-ruler of your Virgo Sun resonates powerfully with your 1st house Saturn in Capricorn, creating an even deeper signature of disciplined, responsible, mastery-oriented identity. Your sense of self is not only Virgoan in its analytical service but deeply Saturnian in its relationship to time, to earned wisdom, and to the understanding that the most important things are built slowly and with immense care. This decan of Virgo is often associated with extraordinary attention to craft — the artisan who spends a lifetime mastering a single medium, not because they cannot do otherwise but because they understand that mastery is the only form of expression worthy of the gift.

### Your Moon: First Decan of Aries (0°00′–9°59′) — Mars Sub-Ruler

Your Moon at 6 degrees and 37 minutes of Aries falls in the first decan, sub-ruled by Mars — which is also the primary ruler of Aries. This means that your Moon is doubly Martian: ruled by Mars as an Aries Moon, and sub-ruled by Mars as a first decan Moon. The emotional quality this creates is one of particularly intense emotional directness, courage, and velocity. There is no ambiguity in this Moon when it comes to feeling and expressing. The fire burns clean. What you feel, you know immediately, and there is very little distance between the feeling and the need to express it. This doubly Martian Moon also speaks to a fierce protectiveness over those you love — a quality of emotional warrior energy that can rise with extraordinary swiftness when the people or principles you care about are threatened. The shadow of this intensely Martian emotional signature is impulsiveness — the risk of acting on emotion before the longer view has been considered. The gift of your Mercury Rx Final Dispositor is precisely this counterbalance: the mind that eventually catches up with the heart and helps channel its fire with precision.

### Your Ascendant: Third Decan of Sagittarius (20°00′–29°59′) — Sun Sub-Ruler

Your Ascendant at 29 degrees and 28 minutes falls in the third decan of Sagittarius, sub-ruled by the Sun in the Chaldean system. The Sun as sub-ruler of your Ascendant adds a quality of solar radiance, personal authority, and the capacity to shine — to be a genuinely central and luminous presence in whatever community or space you inhabit. The third decan of Sagittarius with Solar sub-rulership is associated with the philosopher-king or philosopher-queen archetype: the one who seeks the highest truth AND has the charisma and authority to transmit it to others. This connects beautifully to your Jupiter in Leo as chart ruler — both the decan sub-ruler and the chart ruler bring the Sun’s warmth and authority to bear on your outward expression. You are not merely a seeker of truth. You are a teacher of it. And the best teachers have always been the ones who combined the genuine hunger to know with the warmth and presence to make knowledge feel like an invitation rather than a lecture.
