*365 Days of Astrological Medicine*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

The following extended collection of affirmations is organized by the major themes of the natal chart and designed to be used as a daily practice. Each affirmation is a precise astrological medicine — not a generic positive statement, but a specific antidote to a specific shadow or wound in the chart. Use them in rotation, returning to the ones that feel most uncomfortable. The discomfort is the shadow recognizing itself. The recognition is the beginning of the integration.
