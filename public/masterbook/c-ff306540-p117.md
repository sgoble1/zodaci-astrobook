18C: Room-by-Room Implementation Guide Bedroom (SE or E sector): The bedroom is the most important Feng Shui space in any home, and for April it should be located in the SE or E sector of the residence if at all possible. The elemental quality of the bedroom should be Water dominant — blues, black, deep navy, and dark green in textiles, art, and soft furnishings create the Water element environment that resonates with Kua 1 and activates the Sheng Chi (wealth and prosperity) of the SE direction. All sharp corners — including furniture corners — should be positioned so they do not point directly at the bed, as sharp architectural energy disrupts sleep and the restoration that sleep provides. A pair of objects on the nightstand or dresser (two candles, two crystals, or two small plants in matching containers) activates the partnership energy of Jupiter Leo H7 and the Chiron-Part of Fortune Cancer H7 healing intention. Rose Quartz placed on the nightstand specifically activates the belonging healing of Chiron Cancer H7 — the most important crystal placement for her current healing work. Living Room / Social Space: The living room is where Jupiter Leo H7 should be activated — this is the space of partnership, warmth, expansiveness, and the generous social energy that is the chart ruler's domain. Leo colors (warm gold, amber, deep orange, rich cream) in soft furnishings, art, or decorative objects activate the Jupiter Leo energy. A large mirror placed on the South or East wall expands the space and activates the Fire and Wood elements simultaneously. Living plants — particularly the larger, upright, woody varieties like fiddle-leaf figs or snake plants — add the Wood element circuit completion in the space where social energy is highest. Candles or a working fireplace, used intentionally, activate the Fire element of both the Sagittarius ASC and the Aries Moon, creating the warmth and expansiveness that the chart's Fire elements require in the social domain.
