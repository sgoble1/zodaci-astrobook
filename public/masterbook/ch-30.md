*The Points Between the Points*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

Midpoints are the mathematical midpoints between any two planets or points in the chart. When a third planet or point falls on a midpoint, it activates the combined energy of the two planets whose midpoint it occupies. Midpoints reveal hidden connections and activations that are not visible in the ordinary aspect structure of the chart.

### Sun/Moon Midpoint: The Heart of the Chart

The Sun/Moon midpoint — the midpoint between your Sun at 13°56' Virgo and your Moon at 6°37' Aries — falls at approximately 25°16' Cancer. This is the most personal midpoint in any chart, representing the integration of the conscious self (Sun) and the emotional self (Moon). Your Sun/Moon midpoint in Cancer H7 means that the deepest integration of your conscious and emotional selves happens through the themes of Cancer in the house of partnership: through emotional belonging, through being held, through the experience of home in another person.

### Mercury/Pluto Midpoint

The Mercury/Pluto midpoint — the midpoint between your Mercury at 16°35' Virgo and your Pluto at 15°28' Scorpio — falls at approximately 1°01' Scorpio. ### Venus/Jupiter Midpoint: The Abundance Point
