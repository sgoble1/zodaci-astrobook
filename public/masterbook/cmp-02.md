VII\. Annual Profections — Age 35 (2025–2026)

*Annual profections are one of the oldest timing techniques in astrology
— a system so elegant it requires no technology, only counting. From
birth, the chart moves one house per year. At age 35, we have traveled
35 houses — and the remainder tells us where the year's energy is
focused.*

| **Calculation** | **Result** |
|----|----|
| Age at last birthday (September 6, 2025) | 35 |
| 35 ÷ 12 = | 2 remainder 11 |
| House activated (counting from H1) | **H12 — Twelfth House** |
| H12 sign in Whole Sign Houses (from Sag ASC) | **Scorpio** |
| Traditional Time Lord | **Mars** (traditional ruler of Scorpio) |
| Modern Time Lord | **Pluto** (modern ruler of Scorpio, domicile natal H10) |
| Natal Pluto location | Scorpio 15°28' H10 — in domicile |

**The Twelfth House Profection Year** is one of the most misunderstood
in all of astrology. The 12th house has been called the house of
self-undoing, enemies, isolation, and hidden places — but this is the
shadow interpretation. The depth interpretation: the 12th is the house
of the unconscious made conscious, of the work done in solitude that
becomes the foundation for public emergence, of the dissolution of what
no longer serves before the next first-house year of rebirth.

With Scorpio ruling your 12th (from the Sagittarius Ascendant in Whole
Signs), and with your natal Pluto in domicile Scorpio H10 as the Time
Lord, this year's theme is: *excavation of what is hidden,
transformation of power structures, and the underground work that makes
the next public emergence possible.*

Your age-35 year (September 2025 through September 2026) is a year to go
deep — not to perform, but to excavate. The Pluto H10 Time Lord says:
the hidden material of your career — the work you haven't shown anyone,
the ideas you haven't voiced, the power you haven't yet claimed — all of
it is surfacing now. The 12th house profection year is preparation. The
first house year that follows (age 36, beginning September 2026) will be
the emergence. This year is the root work of what blooms next.
