# The Sabian Symbols — An Illuminated Wheel

*The Sabian Symbols are one of the most mysterious and precise
divination systems in modern astrology — 360 images, one for each degree
of the zodiac, channeled in 1925 by clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler and
astrologer Marc Edmund Jones. Each symbol for your natal placements acts
as a picture that holds more truth than a paragraph of analysis. Read
them slowly. They were waiting for you.*
