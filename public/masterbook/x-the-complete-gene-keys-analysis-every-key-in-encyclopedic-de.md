The Gene Keys system, developed by Richard Rudd and derived from the I Ching through the Human Design System's activation points, maps the journey from shadow-frequency through gift-frequency to what Rudd calls the Siddhi — the highest expression of each archetypal energy. The system is based on the 64 hexagrams of the I Ching, each of which corresponds to a specific codon in the human genome — a specific sequence of DNA that encodes a particular quality of consciousness. The Gene Keys describe the journey of consciousness from its most contracted expression (the shadow) through its most expanded expression (the Siddhi), with the gift as the practical, livable expression of the archetype in daily life.

The Gene Keys system is not a prediction system — it is a contemplation system. The practice of working with the Gene Keys is the practice of sitting with the shadow, the gift, and the Siddhi of each key and allowing the contemplation to reveal the specific way in which the archetype is expressing in one's own life. The shadow is not something to be eliminated; it is something to be understood, integrated, and transformed. The gift is not something to be performed; it is something to be lived, in the specific, particular, irreplaceable way that only this individual can live it. The Siddhi is not something to be achieved; it is something to be received, in the moments of complete clarity when the pattern has fully resolved and the truth has become visible in its entirety.
