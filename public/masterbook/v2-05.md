# Secondary Progressions

Your Evolved Chart in 2026

Secondary progressions operate on one of astrology's most beautiful and
philosophically elegant principles: each day that passes after birth
corresponds to one year of the life. To understand April's progressed
chart as of 2026, the astrologer examines the sky as it appeared 35 days
after September 6, 1990 — which is approximately October 11, 1990. The
planetary positions on that single day carry the progressed chart of the
35-year-old April. Secondary progressions describe the internal
evolution of the personality — not the external events that transits
bring, but the shifts in identity, emotional temperament, and relational
style that happen slowly, from the inside out, like the growth rings of
a tree rather than the weather that changes daily. Progressions answer
the question: who is April becoming, beneath the surface, in this window
of time? What qualities of the natal chart are maturing, shifting, or
arriving at new thresholds? In 2026, several progressed positions are of
extraordinary significance.

Progressed Sun — Approximately 18°-19° Libra, Ninth House

The natal Sun in Virgo H8 has progressed at the rate of approximately
one degree per year, moving through the final degrees of Virgo and into
Libra. At age 35, the progressed Sun sits at approximately 18°-19° Libra
— a sign and house combination that speaks to a profound internal shift
in your core identity. The natal Virgo H8 Sun is the deep analyst: the
one who excavates what is hidden, who holds depth, who operates in the
territory of transformation, shared resources, and the investigation of
what lies beneath the surface. This is a powerful and necessary solar
identity. But the progressed Sun in Libra H9 is something different: the
philosopher, the truth-seeker, the one who takes the depth of H8 and
begins to carry it outward into the wider world through the 9th house's
orientation toward higher learning, teaching, publishing, long-distance
travel, and the construction of a coherent philosophy of life. Libra
adds the quality of justice — of asking what is fair, what creates
balance, what serves the harmony of the larger whole. The progression
says: April's deepest self is currently shifting from the private
depth-worker (H8 Virgo) toward the public wisdom-carrier (H9 Libra).
This is not a loss of depth. It is the application of depth to a wider
canvas. The analytical precision of the Virgo Sun is still present — it
is now being deployed in service of a philosophical and pedagogical
vision rather than purely in service of internal excavation. In
practical terms, this progressed Sun is the energy behind the Process
Guide emergence: the one who has done the internal work and who is now
beginning to build systems and communicate wisdom that serves others as
well as herself.

Progressed Moon — Approximately Capricorn or Aquarius

The progressed Moon moves at approximately 12-13 degrees per year,
cycling through all twelve signs over approximately 27-28 years. At age
35, the progressed Moon has traveled through approximately 420-455
degrees from its natal position at 6°37' Aries — placing it in the
vicinity of late Capricorn or early Aquarius. If the progressed Moon has
entered Aquarius, it is approaching or already conjuncting the natal
North Node at 5°17' Aquarius — which would create a moment of
extraordinary emotional resonance with the soul's destination. A
progressed Moon conjuncting the natal North Node says: the emotional
self is arriving at the soul's chosen evolutionary direction. The
feelings, the intuitions, the emotional needs and responses — all are
orienting toward the Aquarian North Node qualities of authenticity,
individuality, and freedom from performance. Emotionally, this window
feels like: a growing discomfort with the old emotional patterns of the
South Node (performing warmth for approval, giving more than is
sustainable), and a growing attraction to emotional experiences that
feel genuinely authentic — less polished, less managed, more real. The
progressed Moon in Aquarius is the emotional body learning to stop
asking "am I pleasing everyone?" and beginning to ask "is this actually
true for me?" This is one of the most significant internal shifts of the
current period, and it is happening quietly, beneath the surface, even
when the conscious mind is focused on the larger and more dramatic
transits.

Progressed Mercury — Late Virgo or Early Libra

Natal Mercury at 16°35' Virgo was retrograde — which means that in the
days immediately following September 6, 1990, the progressed Mercury
would have continued backward briefly before stationing direct and then
moving forward. By age 35, progressed Mercury has moved approximately
15-20 degrees forward from its stationing point, placing it in late
Virgo or possibly the very first degrees of Libra. If progressed Mercury
has crossed into Libra, the mind is undergoing its own shift parallel to
the Sun: from the purely Virgoan analytical mode (precision, critical,
interior) toward a Libran mode that is more relational, more interested
in balance and beauty and the other's perspective, more oriented toward
dialogue than monologue. The retrograde-turned-direct quality of
progressed Mercury carries specific significance: the mind that was
turned inward for years is now, in this window, genuinely beginning to
turn outward. The volcanic image of the Sabian Symbol is active in the
progressed Mercury as well — the thoughts that have been building and
refining and waiting are now approaching the threshold of external
expression. Writing, teaching, speaking, and communication projects
begun or expanded in this window (2026-2027) carry the energy of the
progressed Mercury station direct: they have the quality of words that
have been a very long time in the making.

Progressed Venus — Virgo

Natal Venus at 29°20' Leo has progressed approximately 35 degrees,
moving through the final minutes of Leo and into Virgo. By 2026,
progressed Venus sits solidly in Virgo — having crossed the Leo/Virgo
cusp and entered the territory of a very different relational mode. The
shift from Leo Venus to Virgo Venus in progression is significant: the
love style is moving from the grand, expressive, dramatically generous
Leo mode toward something more quietly devoted, more practically
attentive, more interested in showing love through acts of precision and
care than through theatrical display. Progressed Venus in Virgo says:
what you are beginning to find genuinely attractive in partnership is
the thoughtful, the considered, the person who pays attention to details
because they care — not the dramatic lover who makes spectacular
gestures but cannot sustain consistent presence. This is also progressed
Venus moving through the same sign as your natal Sun and Mercury — it is
a period in which love and identity are becoming more integrated, in
which who you love and who you are are less separate questions than they
have previously been. The right relationship in this window is not the
one that makes the biggest impression. It is the one that actually fits.
