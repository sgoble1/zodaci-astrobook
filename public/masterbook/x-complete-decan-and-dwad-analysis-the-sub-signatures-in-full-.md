Each sign of the zodiac is divided into three decans of 10 degrees each, and each decan is further divided into dwads (dodecatemoria) of 2.5 degrees each. These sub-divisions reveal the secondary sign influences operating within each planetary placement, adding nuance and specificity to the natal portrait. What follows is a complete decan and dwad analysis for every major placement in April's chart.
