The Sabian Symbols are one of the most mysterious and precise divination systems in modern astrology — 360 images, one for each degree of the zodiac, channeled in 1925 by clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler and astrologer Marc Edmund Jones in Balboa Park, San Diego. The symbols were received in a single day, with Wheeler drawing cards from a shuffled deck while Jones recorded the images she described. The result was a set of 360 symbolic images that have proven, over the decades since their creation, to be among the most precise and illuminating tools in the astrological tradition. What follows is an extended meditation on each of April's natal Sabian Symbols — a sustained contemplation of the image and its personal meaning at maximum depth.
