# The Arabic Parts — Spirit, Fortune & Marriage

*The Arabic Parts (or Lots) are calculated points that combine three
chart factors — the Ascendant, Sun, Moon, or other planets — to reveal
hidden dimensions of the chart. For a Day Chart like yours, the formulas
shift slightly. Three Lots are most essential to your story.*

| **Lot** | **Calculated Position** | **Significance for April** |
|----|----|----|
| **Lot of Fortune** | Cancer 22°08', H7 | Your fortune, flow, and peak vitality are found through the themes of Cancer (emotional depth, nurturing, belonging, the mother-principle) expressed in the house of partnership (H7). You are luckiest when you are emotionally authentic in relationship — when you let yourself be seen and held. Fortune here also suggests your natural luck increases near water, near home environments, near anything that triggers the feeling of safe belonging. |
| **Lot of Spirit** | ~Gemini 6°47', H5 | The Lot of Spirit represents the soul's active purpose — what you are here to do, the direction your will most naturally flows. Gemini 6°47' H5 is conjunct natal Mars at 3°02' (3°45' orb). Your soul's purpose is creative expression, communication, the play of ideas, and the generation of new thought. The fifth house is the house of creativity, play, children, and love affairs — not just romantic ones but love affairs with ideas, projects, and vocations. Your spirit came here to create. Actively, generatively, joyfully. |
| **Lot of Marriage** | ~Leo 9–11°, H7–H8 | The Lot of Marriage falls near your Vertex at Leo 10°38' H8 — the fated encounter point. This is deeply significant: your most fated partnership encounters carry a Leo-H8 quality — they arrive with a sense of recognition, they transform you, they involve someone of genuine creative radiance or self-expressive power. These are not casual connections. When the Lot of Marriage and the Vertex are so close, partnerships arrive with the quality of destiny. They feel inevitable. Whether they are permanent is another question — but they are never accidental. |
