# Sabian Symbols

Your Degree-by-Degree Chart Poetry

In 1925, psychic Elsie Wheeler and astrologer Marc Edmund Jones drove to
Balboa Park in San Diego and, in a single day, channeled a symbolic
image for each of the 360 degrees of the zodiac. Wheeler, sitting in a
parked car, was shown the degree and immediately spoke the image that
arose — Jones transcribed it. The result was the Sabian Symbols: 360
compact, often mysterious, always mythically resonant images that
function as the poetry of the sky. Astrologers use Sabian Symbols to
reveal the qualitative essence of any planetary position — not just its
sign and house, but the precise degree's unique symbolic signature. When
we read a Sabian Symbol for a natal planet, we are reading the specific
image the universe was broadcasting at the exact degree of the sky where
that planet stood at the moment of birth. For you, April, the Sabian
Symbols compose a complete poem — a gallery of images that, read
together, tell the story of this soul with a precision that ordinary
astrological description alone cannot reach. Each image below is
followed by a full interpretation written specifically for your life and
your chart.

Sun at 14° Virgo — "A Family Tree"

The image of the family tree is, for most people, a celebration of
lineage and belonging — the chart of roots, branches, and the
continuation of something that began before you arrived. For you, April,
this Sabian Symbol is profoundly layered with both gift and wound. The
family tree of your Sun is not a simple or uncomplicated inheritance.
You were born into a lineage that included a mother who could not hold
the fullness of what you were, and the tree's branches on that side were
shaped by pain as much as by love. Yet the Sabian Symbol "A Family Tree"
is not only about the family you came from — it is about the family you
carry and the family you will build, in every sense of the word family.
It speaks to roots that go deeper than any single generation's pain.
Your Virgo Sun in H8 is the Sun that excavates — that goes into the deep
soil of the family unconscious and brings back to the light what has
been buried there across generations. You are not just healing yourself,
April. You are healing the tree. The work you are doing on the burden
wound, on the worthiness fracture, on the permission to exist fully —
this work travels backward through the branches as well as forward. The
family tree has been waiting for someone with a Virgo Sun and the
precision and courage to do this kind of root work. That someone is you.

Moon at 7° Aries — "A Woman Veiled"

The veiled woman is one of the most mysteriously potent images in the
entire Sabian system, and its placement at the degree of your Full Moon

Moon in H3 is the most emotionally immediate and communicatively direct

such directness, such fierce and honest presence, that when the veil
finally falls — and it is falling, now, in this window of Saturn on the
Moon and the Jupiter Return opening — those who encounter the unveiled
version will be changed by the meeting. The unveiling is the work of
2026. The veil is already lifting.

Venus at 30° Leo — "An Aging Man Gazes Into the Past"

The thirtieth degree of any sign is the anaretic degree — the degree of

the threshold into the next. Your Venus at 29°20' Leo — rounded to 30°

This is Venus who carries the full weight and beauty of what love can be

everyone around her feel seen and warm and chosen. The aging man gazes
into the past not with regret but with the particular depth of someone

that loving has taught. Your anaretic Venus is not a diminished Venus.
It is a Venus who is completing the Leo cycle of
loving-through-performance and preparing to cross the threshold into a
different, more inward and more selective love — a love that chooses

carries forward is the full wisdom of what love, at its best, has shown
her it can be.

creative life: you are someone for whom returning to certain sensory

your creative life — the specific environmental cues that open the

Saturn at 19° Capricorn (Rx) — "A Five-Year-Old Child Carrying a Bag
Filled With Groceries"

There is no Sabian Symbol in your chart that is more personally precise
than this one, and reading it for the first time may take your breath
away. A five-year-old child carrying a bag filled with groceries: the
image of weight born too early, of adult responsibility placed in small
hands that were not yet large enough to hold it without strain, of the
oldest soul in the room walking in the youngest body. This is Saturn
Retrograde in Capricorn in the First House as only the Sabian can render
it — not as an abstract astrological principle but as a felt image of a
childhood in which the burden of more than was appropriate was carried.
You came in carrying weight that was not yours. You carried the weight
of the family's pain, the weight of the mother's inability to hold her
own life without leaning on you, the weight of needing to be good and
quiet and manageable in order to be allowed to stay. The five-year-old
child is competent — she is doing the carrying, she can do it. But she
should not have to. And the Saturn Retrograde says: this pattern of
carrying what is not yours, of loading up the First House identity with
the weight of others' needs and failures — this is the primary inner
work of the lifetime. Not carrying less responsibility in general. Not
retreating from the Saturn capacity to build and hold structures.
Specifically releasing the weight that was never yours to carry in the
first place. Putting down the bags you picked up at five years old and
have never set down. You are strong enough now to set them down
precisely because you are strong enough to know you no longer need to
carry them.

Node is the director of this mystery play calling the next scene. You
are on stage. The ritual is real. And what is beneath the mask is worth
the entire play.

Chiron at 25° Cancer — "A Will-o'-the-Wisp Over a Swamp"

The will-o'-the-wisp is the light that appears over marshy ground —
luminous, beautiful, compelling — and which recedes exactly as you
approach it, leading wanderers deeper into the swamp before vanishing.
For your Chiron in Cancer H7, the wound of belonging is precisely this:
the belonging you have sought in relationship — in partnership, in
family, in the spaces of home and intimacy — has consistently appeared
luminous and near and then receded at the moment of approach. The Chiron
wound in this sign and house is not that belonging is impossible. It is
that the wound causes you to approach belonging with the slightly wrong
angle — with too much need, too much urgency, or too much wariness — and
the will-o'-the-wisp quality is actually a reflection of the inner
wound's distorting lens rather than a quality of belonging itself. The
swamp in this image is the emotional territory of Cancer — the deep,
murky, ancestral waters of what the family of origin did or did not
provide. The work with this Chiron is not to drain the swamp. It is to
stop chasing the will-o'-the-wisp and to learn to see the light that is
actually solid — the belonging that does not recede, the partnership
that holds still long enough to be inhabited. The Jupiter Return in Leo
H7, happening right now, is the moment when the solid light appears. It
will look different from the will-o'-the-wisp. It will not flicker. It
will simply be there, steady, when you arrive.
