# Tarot Birth Card & Oracle Guidance

The Chariot — Major Arcana VII — Your Birth Card

Life Path 7 reduces directly to The Chariot, the seventh card of the
Major Arcana, through the simplest and most elegant of the Tarot birth
card calculation systems. The Chariot is not a quiet card. It is one of
the most kinetic images in the entire Tarot deck: a figure in armor,
standing upright in a chariot, holding no reins — commanding two
sphinx-like creatures (one black, one white) through pure force of will.
The chariot moves forward not because the two forces beneath it have
been resolved or unified, but because they have been held in directed
tension by a will strong enough to harness what neither force could
accomplish alone. This card was chosen for April with the precision that
only the universe's deeper math can produce.

The Chariot maps to April's chart at the most fundamental structural
level. Her natal chart is a Full Moon chart — the Sun in Virgo opposite
the Moon in Aries, held in exact opposition across the axis of H8 and
H3, the private depths and the public voice. This is the chart's central
tension: the black sphinx and the white sphinx, the inward analyst and
the outward communicator, the hidden depths and the visible expression.
The Chariot does not resolve this tension. The Chariot harnesses it. The
entire power of the Full Moon opposition — the extraordinary creative
and expressive energy generated by holding two opposite positions in the
same chart simultaneously — becomes the chariot's motive force when it
is not being used to fight itself. The Saturn H1 in Capricorn is the
chariot's structure: the discipline and architecture that gives the
opposing forces a container to move within rather than exploding
outward. The Aries Moon is the right-hand sphinx: direct, immediate,
forward-facing, warrior. The Virgo Sun is the left-hand sphinx: precise,
patient, analytical, inward. Together, under the Saturn chariot's
guidance, they produce the kind of forward momentum that does not depend
on favorable external conditions — it depends only on the quality of the
will directing the tension.

The Chariot's armor is significant too. It is not worn because the
wearer is afraid. It is worn because the journey being undertaken is
genuinely significant — it is the armor of the one who knows what is at
stake and has prepared accordingly. April's life — the losses, the
burden encoding, the Amazon years, the PG transition, the healing — has
been the forging of this armor. Not to make her invulnerable, but to
make her capable. The Chariot birth card says: you were born for forward
movement. Not backward glancing, not sideways compromise, not stationary
survival. Forward. And the two forces you have spent thirty-five years
trying to reconcile — the depth and the voice, the analytical self and
the expressive self, the private and the public, the one who knows and
the one who speaks — do not need to be reconciled. They need to be
harnessed. Stop trying to pick one and let the other go. Hold both
reins, even though you hold them without reins. Move forward. The
chariot is already moving.

The Tower — The Secondary Card

In some Tarot birth card systems, when the birth card reduces to a
single digit, it is paired with the double-digit card from the same
number family — and 7 pairs with 16, The Tower. The Tower is the card of
necessary structural collapse: the lightning that strikes the edifice
that was built on false foundations, the sudden and total disruption of
what was believed to be permanent, the fall that turns out to be the
beginning of the most authentic construction that has ever occurred. For
April, The Tower has already come — in the form of the Saturn Return
(2018-2020), in the loss of both parents, in the dismantling of the
identity that was organized around the burden wound's conditions. The
Tower does not arrive gently. But when its work is complete, what
remains is only what was real — only what was genuinely built on truth
rather than on what needed to be performed. The Pluto H8 stellium, the
Saturn Return, the grief, the Pluto transits — all are Tower energy in
April's chart. And what remains, after the lightning, after the fall of
the edifice that was built to survive rather than to thrive — is the
Chariot. The clear ground. The two sphinxes, standing still, waiting for
the will that is strong enough to direct them. This is the moment she is
in. The Tower's work is mostly complete. The Chariot is ready.
