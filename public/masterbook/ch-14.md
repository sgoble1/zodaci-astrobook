Your Midheaven sits at 24 degrees and 59 minutes of Libra — the point in the chart representing public identity, career, legacy, and what you are known for. And sitting just 51 minutes from your Midheaven is Spica, the brightest star in the constellation Virgo and one of the most benefic fixed stars in all of astrology.

### Spica: The Luckiest Star in Astrology

Spica is associated with brilliance, success, artistic talent, and the harvest of extraordinary effort. It is the star of Virgo's wheat sheaf — the reward that comes to those who have done the genuine work of cultivation. You were born with the luckiest career star in the zodiac conjunct your Midheaven. This does not mean effortless — it means that the effort you put into your genuine life's work will be recognized and rewarded in ways that feel, to you, almost disproportionate.

**Spica's blessing is particularly strong for writers, healers, teachers, and those who work with invisible truths. The harvest will come. Trust the growing season.**

### Libra Midheaven: Beauty, Balance, and Justice

The Libra Midheaven describes a public calling oriented toward beauty, balance, fairness, and the art of bringing things into right relationship. Your career reaches its highest expression through beauty, sudden awareness, and the capacity to make people stop and see what they had always been walking past. The Sabian Symbol for Libra 25° is 'The Sight of an Autumn Leaf Brings to a Pilgrim the Sudden Consciousness of Beauty' — your public life is touched by the divine gift of revelation.
