In Chinese astrology, the year of birth determines the animal sign, and the element of that year (Metal, Water, Wood, Fire, or Earth) modifies the animal's qualities. You were born in 1990, the Year of the Metal Horse — the strongest and most enduring expression of the Horse archetype.

### The Horse: Freedom, Beauty, and Endurance

The Horse is the seventh animal in the Chinese zodiac cycle, associated with freedom, beauty, independence, and the capacity for sustained effort over long distances. The Horse is not the fastest animal in the zodiac — that distinction belongs to the Rat — but it is the one that can run the longest, that can sustain effort over distances that would exhaust other animals, that can carry weight without breaking.

The Horse is also associated with beauty — not the delicate beauty of the Rabbit or the regal beauty of the Dragon, but the powerful, kinetic beauty of a creature in full motion, doing exactly what it was built to do. The Horse is most beautiful when it is running, when it is working, when it is fully engaged with the task that calls it forward.

### The Metal Element: Iron Endurance

The Metal element adds iron endurance, structural integrity, and the capacity to sustain effort over the long term to the Horse's natural qualities. Metal is the element of precision, of the blade that cuts cleanly, of the structure that holds its shape under pressure. The Metal Horse is the Horse that runs farther than other Horses, that outlasts what would exhaust them, that shines with a particular quality of earned brilliance.

This is not the Horse of easy victories; it is the Horse of the long race, the one who is still running when others have stopped. The Metal Horse's greatest gift is its capacity to sustain — to keep going when the going is hard, to maintain the quality of its effort even when the conditions are difficult, to arrive at the finish line not just intact but stronger than when it started.
