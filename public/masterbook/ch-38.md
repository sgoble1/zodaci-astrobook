*Structure Given to the Voice*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

Saturn takes approximately 29.5 years to complete one full orbit of the zodiac, spending approximately 2.5 years in each sign. Saturn's transits describe the domain of life where discipline, accountability, and the building of lasting structures are most demanded in each period.

### Saturn in Aries (2025-2028): The Bucket Handle Transit

Saturn entered Aries in May 2025 and will remain in Aries until April 2028. Saturn at approximately 13-14° Aries in June 2026 is approaching your natal Moon at 6°37' Aries in your third house — it has been in the general territory for months, and as it completes its pass and begins its retrograde cycles, it will make exact or near-exact contact with your natal Moon in Aries multiple times between 2026 and 2027.

This is the Saturn transit of the bucket handle. Saturn pressing on the Aries Moon means: everything the Moon has wanted to impulsively express, everything the first wound told you to suppress, everything the Full Moon birth illuminated — Saturn is now asking you to give it structure and deliberateness. This is not a blocking transit. This is Saturn strengthening the handle. What you communicate now carries lasting weight.

### Saturn in Taurus (2028-2030): Building the Foundation

Saturn in Taurus will transit your second house of values and resources, bringing a period of financial consolidation and the building of material security. The structures of value that Saturn in Taurus demands are not just financial — they are also about clarifying what you truly value, what is worth building, and what is worth releasing. This is the period when the platform built in Phase 5 and 6 receives its financial and material foundation.

### Saturn in Gemini (2030-2032): The Voice Finds Its Structure

Saturn in Gemini will transit your third house of communication, writing, and local environment — the same house as your natal Moon. This is a significant transit for the bucket handle: Saturn in the third house demands that the communication be structured, disciplined, and built for permanence. This is the period when the writing, the teaching, the speaking — whatever form the bucket handle's expression has taken — receives the structure that will allow it to last.
