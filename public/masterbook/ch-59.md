*Words for Your Stars*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

The following affirmations are written specifically for your natal chart — not generic positive statements, but precise astrological medicine for the specific wounds and gifts of your specific configuration. Use them daily, especially the ones that feel most uncomfortable. The discomfort is the shadow recognizing itself.

### For the Aries Moon H3 (The Bucket Handle)

> • My emotional expression is not a burden. It is the instrument through which my entire chart speaks.
>
> • I am allowed to feel what I feel, immediately and completely, without apology or management.
>
> • My directness is not aggression. It is emotional courage. It is the Aries Moon doing exactly what it was built to do.
>
> • When I speak my truth, I am not being too much. I am being exactly enough.
>
> • The bucket handle is open. The engine runs. My voice is the exhaust valve of my entire chart.

### For the Virgo Sun H8 (The Sacred Analyst)

> • My depth is not morbidity. It is my natural habitat. The most important truths live below the waterline, and I was built to find them.
>
> • My analytical precision is a gift, not a burden. I see what others cannot see because I was built to see it.
>
> • I am a truth-excavator. I go into the darkness and come back with something true. This is sacred work.
>
> • My identity is forged in transformation. I emerge from my most difficult periods more fundamentally me than before.

### For the Galactic Center Ascendant

> • I am a cosmic receiver and transmitter of truth. The information that arrives in me is meant to be trusted and shared.
>
> • I am not too much. I am tuned to a frequency that most people have not yet learned to hear.
>
> • The messages I carry are meant for a frequency beyond the immediate and the convenient. I trust them.
>
> • My presence, when I am fully in it, has a quality of grace that others feel in ways they cannot explain. I allow this.

### For the Saturn Rx H1 (The Weight-Bearer)

> • I am aging into power. The qualities that made early life feel heavy are becoming the very qualities that produce earned authority.
>
> • The bag is still there, but my arms no longer tremble. I am strong enough. More than strong enough.
>
> • I was not born to be small. I was born to carry weight and to build something that lasts.
>
> • My seriousness is not sadness. It is depth. It is the mark of someone being prepared for a weight-bearing life.

### For the Leo Venus H8 (Love at the Crisis Degree)

> • I deserve the same quality of love I so freely give. This is not a preference. This is the answer.
>
> • I am worthy of being adored — not in a shallow way, but in the way that the deepest self wants to be seen and celebrated in its full radiance.
>
> • I do not settle for someone who can only do one. I need the partner who can hold my Aries Moon's fierce emotional honesty AND meet my Leo Venus's need for celebration and devotion.
>
> • My love is not excessive. It is complete. And I deserve completeness in return.

### For the Pluto H10 (The Apex of Destiny)

> • I cannot have an invisible career. My presence in a professional context is itself a catalyzing force that changes the culture, the process, the people around me.
>
> • The warehouse floor is not the opposite of my destiny — it is the beginning of it. Everything that comes next is built on what I am building right now.
>
> • What I do in the world will not be small. Pluto in domicile in the tenth house does not do small.
>
> • I am here, in part, to address what was unjust. What is hidden will surface through my career. What was silent will speak through my voice.

### For the North Node Aquarius H1 (The Soul's Compass)

> • I am becoming my destiny. The self that was promised is arriving in the body.
>
> • My purpose is to be authentically myself, visibly and without apology, in a way that serves the collective.
>
> • I am not lost. I am standing at the threshold of the most significant opening of my entire life.
>
> • The preparation is complete. The door is open. I step through.
