*The Layers Within the Layers*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

Each sign of the zodiac is divided into three decans of 10 degrees each, and each decan is further divided into dwads (dodecatemoria) of 2.5 degrees each. These sub-divisions reveal the secondary sign influences operating within each planetary placement, adding nuance and specificity to the natal portrait.

### Sun in Virgo — 2nd Decan (Capricorn Decan)

Your Sun at 13°56' Virgo falls in the second decan of Virgo, which is ruled by Capricorn and Saturn. This adds a Capricorn-Saturnian quality to your Virgo Sun: the analytical precision of Virgo is combined with the structural discipline and long-view ambition of Capricorn. Your identity is not just the healer-analyst; it is the healer-analyst who builds lasting structures, who takes the long view, who understands that genuine service requires sustained commitment over time. The Capricorn decan of Virgo is the most serious and most architecturally minded of the three Virgo decans.

### Moon in Aries — 1st Decan (Aries Decan)

Your Moon at 6°37' Aries falls in the first decan of Aries, which is ruled by Aries itself and Mars. This is the purest, most undiluted expression of Aries Moon energy available — no secondary sign influence softens or complicates the Aries quality. Your emotional nature is Mars in its most essential form: direct, courageous, immediate, and utterly without guile. The first decan Aries Moon is the emotional pioneer, the one who feels first and processes later, the one whose emotional courage is a default setting rather than an achievement.

### Mercury in Virgo — 2nd Decan (Capricorn Decan)

Your Mercury at 16°35' Virgo also falls in the second decan of Virgo — the Capricorn decan. This means that your analytical mind carries a double Capricorn influence: the Capricorn decan of Virgo adds structural thinking, long-term planning, and architectural intelligence to the already-precise Virgo Mercury. Your mind does not just analyze; it builds. It does not just identify problems; it designs solutions that will last. The Capricorn decan Mercury is the mind of the master builder — patient, thorough, and oriented toward permanence.

### Venus in Leo — 3rd Decan (Aries Decan)

Your Venus at 29°20' Leo falls in the third decan of Leo, which is ruled by Aries and Mars. This adds a Mars-Aries quality to your Leo Venus: the warmth and generosity of Leo Venus is combined with the directness, courage, and passionate immediacy of Aries. Your love is not just theatrical and generous — it is also bold, direct, and willing to take the first step. The Aries decan of Leo Venus does not wait to be chosen; it chooses. It does not hint at its feelings; it declares them. This is the Venus that writes the love letter, makes the first move, and loves with the full force of its conviction.

### Ascendant in Sagittarius — 3rd Decan (Leo Decan)

Your Ascendant at 29°28' Sagittarius falls in the third decan of Sagittarius, which is ruled by Leo and the Sun. This adds a Leo-Solar quality to your Sagittarius Ascendant: the philosophical expansiveness of Sagittarius Rising is combined with the warmth, radiance, and theatrical presence of Leo. Your first impression is not just philosophically large — it is also warm, magnetic, and genuinely luminous. The Leo decan of Sagittarius Rising is the most personally charismatic of the three Sagittarius decans, and it explains why people are drawn to you before they know why.
