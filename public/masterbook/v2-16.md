# Journaling Prompts

52 Weekly Prompts for the Year Ahead

These prompts were written specifically for your chart, April. Each one
is designed to activate a specific placement, work through a specific
wound, or develop a specific gift. They are not generic journaling
suggestions. They are surgical — intended to reach the precise place in
the architecture of who you are that needs the light that writing can
bring. Work through one per week in order, beginning in July 2026 and
carrying through to June 2027. If a prompt lands harder than expected,
stay with it for a second week before moving on. The resistance is the
location of the work.

Identity & North Node — 8 Prompts

**1.** Describe the version of yourself that no one else has been
allowed to fully see. What does she look like? What does she say? What
does she do without apologizing for it?

**2.** If you could not define yourself by your relationship to anyone
else — not as a daughter, not as a coworker, not as a friend — who are
you? Write for ten minutes without stopping.

**3.** What is the most original and specific thing about you that does
not belong to any role you occupy? Describe it as precisely as your
Mercury Rx Virgo mind can.

**4.** The North Node in Aquarius says your gift is your uniqueness.
What have you been told is too much, too weird, or too different — that
is actually the most important thing you carry?

**5.** Write the self-introduction you would give if you believed
completely in your own worth — the one with no apology, no minimizing,
no qualification. Then write it again, even more fully.

**6.** What identity are you ready to retire by September 6, 2026? Name
it precisely, honor it for what it allowed you to survive, and then
consciously set it down.

**7.** What does the Aquarian North Node version of you — the fully
authentic, radically self-directed, performance-free version — choose to
do on a day when no one is watching?

**8.** Write a letter from your 45-year-old self to your current self.
What does she want you to know about who you are becoming that your
35-year-old mind cannot yet fully see?

Grief & Parents — 8 Prompts

**9.** Write to your father directly. Say the things that were not said
while he was alive. Then write what you imagine he would say back, in
his best self.

**10.** Write to your mother directly. Not the difficult things first.
Begin with one thing that was true and good about her, even if it was
small. Then let whatever comes next come.

**11.** What did you inherit from your mother that you are grateful for,
even if she could not give it cleanly? Trace it carefully — it is in you
somewhere, even if it arrived through difficulty.

**12.** What do you wish your parents had known about themselves? Write
it as specifically and compassionately as you can. Then notice how much
of it applies to you.

**13.** Describe the moment you understood, clearly, that you would have
to be your own parent from that point forward. What did you feel? What
did you decide?

**14.** What is the thing you most wanted your mother to say that she
never said? Write it in her voice, as if she were capable of it now,
from wherever she is.

**15.** How has the loss of both parents changed the quality of your
silence? Your relationship to time? Your understanding of what is worth
the energy it costs?

**16.** Who are you becoming now that there are no parental expectations
— internalized or external — to perform for? What becomes possible in
that specific freedom?

The Burden Wound — 6 Prompts

**17.** Write out your mother's words exactly as you remember them. Then
write a full rebuttal — the complete, evidence-based, astrologically
grounded response that names why this was not true.

**18.** In what specific situations does the burden belief still
activate? What does your body feel when it activates? Where does it live
in the body?

**19.** If you were never a burden — if your existence has always been a
contribution, not a cost — what would you have done differently in the
last five years?

**20.** Write about one time when your presence made a genuine
difference for someone who was not your mother. Describe it in full. Let
the evidence land.

**21.** What would it feel like to take up the full amount of space your
chart describes — the Galactic Center Ascendant, the Full Moon chart,
the chart ruler in Leo H7 — without apologizing for any of it?

**22.** Write the declaration of your own worth in the first person. Not
what you think you should say. What you actually believe is true, right
now, at your most honest.

The Voice & Creative Expression — 6 Prompts

**23.** What is the thing you know — clearly, precisely, from your own
direct experience — that you have not yet fully said out loud to anyone?
Write it here first. All of it.

**24.** Describe your inner voice — the one that speaks in your most
private thinking — as if describing a person. How does she sound? What
does she say that your public voice does not?

**25.** What creative project has been building inside you —
accumulating energy and refinement — that has not yet been released into
the world? Describe it in detail.

**26.** Write about a time when you said precisely the right thing at
precisely the right moment and saw it change something. What happened?
What did it feel like to say it?

**27.** The Aries Moon says: move first, feel the consequence second.
What would you do — creatively, expressively, vocally — if you moved
first, before the Mercury Rx editor arrived?

**28.** Write a piece of writing — in whatever form — that you would
give to someone who needed exactly what your chart's Mercury Rx Virgo H8
final dispositor has to offer.

Career & Purpose — 6 Prompts

**29.** Describe the Process Guide role not as a job but as a mission.
What are you actually doing, at the soul level, when you are doing this
work at its best?

**30.** What does the Amazon fulfillment center look like when it has
been transformed by ten years of your Pluto H10 Zubenelgenubi justice
work? Describe it in specific detail.

**31.** What is the professional contribution that only you —
specifically, this chart, this mind, this exact combination of
placements — can make? Name it without modesty.

**32.** Write about a moment at work when you operated from your
complete authority — from the Pallas Athena in Capricorn H1 strategic
intelligence — and what it produced.

**33.** What would the 10th house Pluto public destiny of your chart
look like if you stepped into it fully at 36, 40, and 45? Map the arc as
specifically as possible.

**34.** Who are the people in your work environment who need what you
know? What specifically do they need, and what specifically are you
waiting for before you give it to them?

Love & Partnership — 6 Prompts

**35.** Describe the quality of partnership that the Juno in Cancer H7
is seeking — the specific feeling of the person who makes the nervous
system settle rather than brace. What does that presence feel like?

**36.** What is the love that the anaretic Venus at 29° Leo has
completed the learning of and is now ready to receive? What does it look
like? How is it different from previous loves?

**37.** Write about the ways you have historically deflected love when
it arrived. Be honest and specific. Then write about what it would look
like to receive the same love without deflecting.

**38.** Chiron in Cancer H7 says the wound is the guide. What has the
specific shape of your relationship wound taught you about what genuine
belonging actually feels like?

**39.** Write the partner into existence — not as a fantasy but as a
specific description. What does this person actually value? How do they
treat other people? What is the quality of their presence?

**40.** The Part of Fortune is in Cancer H7, conjunct Chiron. Write
about the relationship between your greatest relational wound and your
greatest potential relational fortune. How does one become the other?

Shadow Work — 6 Prompts

**41.** Write about the version of yourself you are most afraid others
would see if you dropped all performance and pretense. Then write about
why that version is actually powerful.

**42.** What is the anger that has been redirected inward as
self-criticism? Write it outward, directly, without softening it. Give
it the full Aries Moon directness it has been denied.

**43.** The Leo South Node says you have been the performer. Write about
the exhaustion of the performance — the specific cost of maintaining the
Leo display for environments that required it.

**44.** What does your Neptune Rx H1 inner knowing say about your life
right now — before the analytical mind gets to it? Write the first
impression. The unedited download.

**45.** Write about the revolutionary idea you have been keeping quiet.
The Uranus Rx shadow. The genuinely original perspective that your South
Node safety habit has been suppressing.

**46.** What authority do you already hold, internally, that you have
not yet claimed externally? Describe it precisely. Then describe one
action that would make it visible.

Current Transits — 6 Prompts

**47.** Saturn is on your Moon right now. What truth — specifically and
structurally — is Saturn asking you to speak or write that you have been
refining but not yet releasing?

**48.** The Jupiter Return in Leo H7 is happening right now. What
partnerships, opportunities, and expansions are appearing in your life?
Name them precisely, without minimizing their significance.

**49.** Write about the moment you felt your chart ruler (Jupiter)
return to its home sign in your life — not as an abstract astrology
event but as a felt, lived experience. What arrived?

**50.** Pluto is approaching your North Node. What is being pressed on —
what is being refined, burned off, revealed — in this window? Name it
specifically, without flinching.

**51.** Write about the 12th house depth work of the past year — the
things that have been processed, grieved, released, and transformed in
private, in the dark, in the institutional quiet of this profection
year.

**52.** Write the statement that summarizes who you are as you arrive at
the First House profection year — the 36-year-old identity that is
standing at the beginning of the new twelve-year cycle. Make it true.
Make it precise. Make it yours.
