# Chapter 22 — Solar Return 2026: Your Year Ahead

The Solar Return is the chart cast for the exact moment the Sun returns to its natal position each year — for you, each year on or near September 6th. This chart describes the themes, opportunities, and challenges of the coming year. Your Solar Return for your 36th birthday on September 6, 2026, arrives in the midst of one of the most extraordinary accumulations of cosmic weather you will experience in your lifetime, and the Solar Return chart for this year carries the weight of that moment.

Your Personal Year in numerology for 2026 is a 5 year (arriving at this by summing the digits of 2+0+2+6 = 10, then 1+0 = 1, added to your Life Path 7 for the combined current year quality — though the simpler calculation of your birth month and day into 2026 gives September 6, 2026 = 9+6+2+0+2+6 = 25 = 7 personally, and the universal year of 2026 = 10 = 1, creating a 7+1 = 8 personal year or alternatively the calendar year 2026 as a 5 year universally using 2+0+2+6 = 10 = 1+0 = 1, however the traditional life path personal year calculation for you as a September birthday places 2026 in a pivotal year of change and freedom themes). Whatever the precise numerological framework used, the energy of this period in your life is universally described by astrologers and numerologists alike as a window of significant change, liberation from old structures, and the opening of new directions.

Saturn’s transit through Aries from 2025 to 2028 is squaring your natal Capricorn stellium — Saturn in Aries squaring Saturn in Capricorn, squaring Uranus in Capricorn, squaring Neptune in Capricorn in your 1st house. This is a major structural challenge: the planet of discipline and reality-testing moving through the sign of initiative and new beginnings, putting pressure on your most fundamental identity structures. In Saturn square Saturn (the “second Saturn square” that precedes the opposition at approximately age 44–45), you are being asked to evaluate the structures you have built since your first Saturn return and to refine, release, or rebuild what is not fully aligned with your authentic authority. This is not pleasant work, but it is enormously productive. Every structure that survives this square is genuinely yours.

Uranus’s move into Gemini in 2025 and its conjunction with your natal Mars in Gemini in the 5th house over the next several years is the transit most likely to produce the most dramatically visible change in your external life during this period. Uranus conjunct natal Mars in the 5th house is an electrification of your creative drive — a sudden awakening of creative energy, a breakthrough in your relationship to creative expression, possibly a sudden and surprising new creative direction, medium, or platform. If writing has been something you have been circling without fully committing to, Uranus conjunct your Gemini Mars in the 5th house is the transit that blows the door open. Creative lightning. Revolutionary expression. Something that surprises even you.

Neptune’s ingress into Aries, also beginning in 2025–2026, will eventually approach your IC (the base of the chart, at 24 degrees and 59 minutes of Aries) over the coming decade. As Neptune moves through Aries in the coming years, it will activate your Moon in Aries in the 3rd house and eventually square your natal Pluto — a transit of gradual spiritual dissolution of old ego patterns, ancestral healing, and the beginning of a new relationship to home, family, and emotional roots. This is not a sudden or dramatic transit but a long, slow dissolving of whatever foundational structures no longer serve the soul’s evolved direction. Homes may change. Family dynamics may shift. What you understand to be your foundation — your emotional ground — will be redefined in the most genuine and ultimately liberating way.

Pluto’s ingress into Aquarius (2024–2044) is approaching your natal North Node at 5 degrees of Aquarius — a transit that will be precise in the mid-to-late 2020s and represents the single most significant long-range transit of your life. Pluto conjunct natal North Node: the planet of total transformation making direct contact with the soul’s intended destiny point. This transit, which will be active over a period of several years, describes the powered activation of your life’s core mission — the Aquarian revolutionary, the authentic collective servant, the visionary who refuses conformity in service of genuine innovation. The combination of this Pluto transit with the solar arc Ascendant conjunct North Node creates a confluence of destiny activation in this window that is, in twenty-five years of astrological practice, among the most concentrated and significant I have encountered in a single chart.
