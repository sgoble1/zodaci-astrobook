# Chapter 20 — Secondary Progressions: Your Evolved Chart

Secondary progressions are one of astrology’s most elegant timing techniques, operating on the symbolic equivalence of one day’s planetary motion after birth to one year of lived life. In other words, the chart cast for the day after you were born — September 7, 1990 — describes, symbolically, your first year of life. The chart cast for ten days after your birth describes your tenth year. The chart cast for thirty-five days after your birth describes your thirty-fifth year — the one you are now in. Secondary progressions show the slow, inward evolution of the natal planets: the gradual shifts in the quality of your experience that happen from the inside out rather than through external events.

The most significant and frequently consulted progressed point is the progressed Sun, which moves approximately one degree per year. Your natal Sun is at 13 degrees and 56 minutes of Virgo. By age 35, your progressed Sun has moved approximately 35 degrees forward from its natal position, placing it in approximately 18 to 19 degrees of Libra. This means you have been living under a progressed Sun in Libra for several years now, and will continue to do so until your progressed Sun moves into Scorpio in the late 2020s. The progressed Sun in Libra marks a chapter of life in which the solar themes shift from the purely analytical and service-oriented quality of Virgo toward themes of relationship, balance, beauty, justice, and the artful navigation of the one-on-one encounter. Many people with Virgo Suns find that their progressed Sun’s passage through Libra brings significant partnerships to the fore, heightens aesthetic sensibility, and generates a period in which questions of fairness, equality, and the give-and-take of reciprocal relationship become the central curriculum. This resonates with the broader relational themes visible throughout your chart in this window.

As the progressed Sun approaches Scorpio — which will occur in the late 2020s — a powerful new chapter opens: the progressed solar entry into Scorpio marks the beginning of approximately thirty years in which the Sun’s evolved quality is deeply Plutonian — intense, investigative, psychological, and oriented toward the kind of total transformation that your natal 8th house already knows intimately. The progressed Sun in Scorpio, for someone with your natal Pluto in Scorpio in the 10th house, will be extraordinarily potent: a thirty-year period in which the evolved identity moves fully into its most powerful, most complex, and most professionally impactful expression. You are currently in the preparation for this chapter. The Libra Sun years are teaching you the relational wisdom and the capacity for balanced, beautiful expression that the Scorpio Sun years will alchemize into transformative power.

The progressed Moon, which moves approximately one degree per month and completes a full zodiac cycle every twenty-seven to twenty-nine years, provides the most granular timing information in the progressed chart. The progressed Moon’s house position at any given time describes the emotional chapter of life most alive in that period. Its transits through your natal houses activate each house’s themes with a quality of emotional immediacy. When the progressed Moon moves through your 8th house, expect deep psychological processing, transformative encounters, and a pull toward the occult and the deeply researched. When it moves through your 3rd house, your Moon handle is activated and communicative expression flows most abundantly. Tracking the progressed Moon through your life’s chapters gives you an extraordinary emotional map of where you have been and where you are going.
