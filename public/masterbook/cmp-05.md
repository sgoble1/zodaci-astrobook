# Chart Sect & Planetary Condition

*Sect — the ancient Hellenistic principle of day and night charts —
tells us which planets are most constructive and which are most
challenging in your nativity. Your chart is a **Day Chart**: the Sun was
above the horizon at 3:38 PM when you were born, meaning you are a
"solar being," oriented toward visibility, intention, and structured
development.*
