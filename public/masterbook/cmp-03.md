# Fixed Stars & Their Gifts

*Fixed stars operate like concentrated beacons — ancient frequencies
that have been accumulating meaning for millennia. When one touches a
natal planet, it does not merely color that planet: it amplifies it,
crystallizes it, and charges it with the entire mythic history of that
star. You have been marked by four of the most significant stars in the
western tradition. Read this section with reverence.*
