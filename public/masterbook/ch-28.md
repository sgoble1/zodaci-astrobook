Antiscia are the mirror degrees of the zodiac — the points that are equidistant from the solstice axis (0° Cancer/Capricorn). When two planets are in antiscia to each other, they are in a hidden, shadow relationship — a secret conversation that operates beneath the threshold of the ordinary aspects. Contra-antiscia are the mirror degrees of the equinox axis (0° Aries/Libra).
