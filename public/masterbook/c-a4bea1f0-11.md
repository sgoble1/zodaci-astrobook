Annual Strategies

September 6 is not merely a birthday. It is the Solar Return — the precise moment each year when the Sun returns to 13°56' Virgo, the degree it occupied at the instant of April's birth. At this moment, the Sun "comes home" to its natal position, and a new twelve-month solar chapter begins. The Solar Return chart — drawn for wherever April is physically located on her birthday at the moment the Sun perfects its return — describes the predominant themes, opportunities, and challenges of the year ahead. This is a living tool, not a fixed destiny: the

Solar Return chart shifts based on location, which means traveling to a different city for one's birthday can meaningfully change the year's themes. This is a genuine technique used by working astrologers, and for someone with April's chart, it is worth considering. What house does the Solar Return Ascendant fall in? What planets are prominent in the Solar Return? These questions open a rich annual planning conversation with the chart.

The Full Moon nearest to September 6 each year carries a quality of special personal significance that most birthday celebrations overlook. This Full Moon — happening within two weeks of her birthday, with the Sun either in Virgo or just entering Libra — activates the Sun-Moon opposition of her birth chart in a particularly resonant way. The Aries-Virgo axis or the Libra-Aries axis lights up annually at the time of year when she is most naturally reflective. Making

September's Full Moon a deliberate moment of ritual — reviewing the year, acknowledging what was built and what was released, setting the intention for the year ahead — gives the annual cycle a closing-and-opening ceremony that her Virgo Sun H8 and Aries Moon H3 can both fully inhabit.
