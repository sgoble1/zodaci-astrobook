Her Authority is Emotional, which adds a crucial layer to the Wait to Respond strategy. Emotional Authority means that her Sacral responses are not immediately actionable — she must also wait for her emotional wave to complete its cycle before committing. This sounds like: she receives an opportunity, her gut lights up, and she does not say yes immediately. She sleeps on it. She lets the initial excitement settle. She notices how she feels about it the next day, and the day after that. If the yes persists across the emotional wave — if it remains yes when she is in the low of the wave as well as the high — it is a genuine yes she can commit to. If the yes was only a high-wave response, it will fade when the wave drops, and she will have saved herself from a commitment made from excitement alone. The combination of Sacral + Emotional Authority makes April someone whose most important decisions deserve more than 24 hours — not weeks or months, but at least a full emotional cycle, which can be anywhere from days to a week. Her Profile is 3/5 — the Martyr/Heretic — one of the most distinctive and impactful profiles in the Human Design system. Line 3 people are often judged for their "failures," not understanding that the failures are the education — that every relationship that ended, every job that did not last, every attempt that produced information rather than success, has been building the empirical knowledge base that Line 3 requires.
