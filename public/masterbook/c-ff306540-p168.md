Ancestral Patterns The fourth house in a natal chart is the house of family of origin — of the root system from which the person grew, of the emotional and energetic inheritance that was transmitted through blood and proximity and the daily texture of being raised by specific people in a specific environment. In April's chart, the fourth house has Aries on its cusp, ruled by Mars in Gemini in the fifth house. This configuration describes a family of origin that was energetically fast-moving, direct in its communication style (even when the directness was not emotionally safe), characterized by a certain unmediated quality of expression, and organized around themes of speed, independence, and an underlying restlessness. The Aries family cusp is not a gentle or quiet family template. It is a family in motion, a family where things were said and done with urgency, where emotional expression tended toward the direct and the unfiltered, and where the pace of the household left some things — particularly the deeper emotional undercurrents — moving too quickly to be fully processed.
