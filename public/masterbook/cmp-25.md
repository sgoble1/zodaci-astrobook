# Venus Retrograde Personal Guide

*Venus goes retrograde approximately every 18 months, spending about 40
days moving backward through the sky. For your chart — with Venus at the
anaretic 29°20' Leo H8, conjunct Regulus, and entangled with the South
Node Leo H7 — Venus Retrogrades are never ordinary events. They are, for
you, major reckonings with the nature of love, beauty, value, and the
cost of performing either.*

Venus Retrograde in Leo — The Most Significant of All

Venus Retrograde occurs in Leo approximately once every 8 years. The
most recent Venus Rx in Leo was **July 22 – September 3, 2023**, when
Venus stationed retrograde at 28°36' Leo and moved backward to 12°12'
Leo before stationing direct.

**What was activated in Summer 2023:**

- Venus stationed retrograde at 28°36' Leo — within 0°44' of your natal
  Venus at 29°20' Leo. This is an extraordinarily close activation:
  Venus turned backward almost exactly on your natal Venus.

- This meant your natal Venus experienced what is called a **"Venus
  Return Retrograde"** — an especially intense and karmic version of the
  Venus Return cycle. Love patterns from the deepest past surfaced.

- The South Node Leo H7 themes were activated fully: performances of
  love that had been running were exposed. Relationships that were built
  on the Leo South Node pattern of magnetism rather than authenticity
  were either transformed or released.

- The Regulus warning — avoid revenge — was particularly relevant in the
  summer of 2023. If there were moments of wanting to punish, to wound
  back, to use the regal Leo power destructively: note them. They are
  the shadow of what the Venus Retrograde was clearing.

- Your Venus in H8 means the retrograde's deepest work was interior and
  transformative rather than obvious and social. What happened in your
  inner life about love in Summer 2023 was more significant than what
  happened externally.

General Venus Retrograde Navigation for Your Chart

During any Venus Retrograde, your H8 Venus is being activated — because
H8 is the house of deep review, of endings, of what lies beneath the
surface of value and love. Venus Retrograde, for you, is always an
invitation into depth rather than surface. The questions to sit with
during any Venus Rx period:

- What is the value I am performing and what is the value I actually
  hold?

- Where is Regulus's warning relevant — where am I being tempted to use
  my beauty or magnetism in the service of revenge or small power?

- What is the Venus Leo H8 truth about what I genuinely love — not what
  I have been loving because it was impressive, or safe, or familiar?

- Is the current expression of love in my life aligned with the North
  Node Aquarius destination — two fully authentic people, fully
  themselves, choosing each other freely?
