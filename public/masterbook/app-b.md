*Key Concepts Defined*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

| **Term** | **Definition** |
|:---|:---|
| Anaretic Degree | The 29th degree of any sign — the degree of culmination, urgency, and ancient completion. Carries the full accumulated wisdom of the sign and stands at the threshold of the next sign. |
| Annual Profections | An ancient timing technique in which the natal chart moves one house per year, activating the themes of that house and its ruling planet (the Time Lord) for the coming year. |
| Antiscia | Mirror degrees of the zodiac equidistant from the solstice axis (0° Cancer/Capricorn). Planets in antiscia are in a hidden, shadow relationship — a secret conversation beneath the threshold of ordinary aspects. |
| Arabic Parts (Lots) | Calculated points that combine three chart factors to reveal hidden dimensions of the chart. The most important are the Lot of Fortune, the Lot of Spirit, and the Lot of Marriage. |
| Ascendant (ASC) | The degree of the zodiac rising over the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. Describes the soul's approach to living, the first face turned toward the world, and the instinctual stance toward experience. |
| Bucket Chart | A chart shape in which the majority of planets cluster on one side of the chart while a single planet — the handle — stands alone on the opposite side, functioning as the focal point through which the entire chart must be expressed. |
| Chart Ruler | The planet that rules the sign on the Ascendant. For Sagittarius Rising, the chart ruler is Jupiter. The chart ruler governs the overall tone and trajectory of the life's expression. |
| Chiron | An asteroid discovered in 1977, associated with the 'wounded healer' archetype. Describes the central wound of the lifetime and simultaneously the place where the soul's greatest healing gift resides. |
| Day Chart | A chart in which the Sun is above the horizon at the time of birth. In Hellenistic astrology, day charts have different sect assignments than night charts, affecting which planets are most constructive. |
| Decan | A subdivision of each zodiac sign into three sections of 10 degrees each. Each decan is associated with a secondary sign that modifies the qualities of the primary sign. |
| Descendant (DSC) | The degree of the zodiac setting over the western horizon at the moment of birth. Describes the qualities sought in significant others and the mirror of the self in relationship. |
| Dispositor | The planet that rules the sign occupied by another planet. The final dispositor is the planet that, when you follow the chain of rulership from every planet in the chart, you eventually arrive at. |
| Domicile | A planet in the sign it rules — its home sign. A planet in domicile operates with maximum ease, native fluency, and exceptional precision. Mercury in Virgo and Saturn in Capricorn are both in domicile. |
| Dwad (Dodecatemoria) | A subdivision of each decan into sections of 2.5 degrees each. Each dwad is associated with a specific sign that adds further nuance to the planetary placement. |
| Eclipse | A Solar Eclipse occurs when the New Moon aligns at or near the lunar nodes. A Lunar Eclipse occurs when the Full Moon aligns at or near the lunar nodes. Eclipses that fall near natal planets or angles activate those placements with extraordinary intensity. |
| Final Dispositor | The planet that, when you follow the chain of rulership from every planet in the chart, you eventually arrive at. In April's chart, the final dispositor is Mercury Rx in Virgo H8. |
| Fixed Stars | Stars that appear fixed in the sky relative to the planets. When a fixed star conjuncts a natal planet or angle, it amplifies and crystallizes that placement with the entire mythic history of the star. |
| Full Moon Birth | A birth that occurs when the Sun and Moon are in exact opposition — the peak of the lunar cycle. Full Moon births are associated with heightened emotional intensity, maximum illumination, and a particular gift for bringing things to completion and revelation. |
| Galactic Center | The rotational point of the Milky Way galaxy — the supermassive black hole at our galaxy's core, Sagittarius A\*, around which all four hundred billion stars of our galaxy revolve. In astrology, a point of cosmic transmission and universal intelligence. |
| Gene Keys | A synthesis system developed by Richard Rudd, derived from the I Ching through the Human Design System's activation points. Maps the journey from shadow-frequency through gift-frequency to the Siddhi — the highest expression of each archetypal energy. |
| Harmonic Charts | Charts derived by multiplying all natal positions by a given number. The 7th harmonic chart reveals the themes of fate, karma, and the mysterious arrangements that seem to transcend ordinary causality. |
| House | One of twelve divisions of the natal chart, each governing a specific domain of life. The houses are determined by the Ascendant and describe the arenas in which the planetary energies play out. |
| Human Design | A synthesis system that combines astrology, the I Ching, the Kabbalah, and the Hindu chakra system to create a detailed map of how an individual's energy works — how they are designed to make decisions, interact with others, and fulfill their purpose. |
| Jupiter Return | The transit that occurs when Jupiter returns to its natal position — approximately every 12 years. For April, with Jupiter as chart ruler in H7, the Jupiter Return is one of the most significant transits of the life cycle. |
| Kua Number | A number derived from the birth year in Chinese metaphysics, used in Feng Shui to determine auspicious and inauspicious directions for the individual. April's Kua Number is 1 (Water). |
| Life Path Number | The most fundamental numerological indicator of the soul's purpose, derived from the birth date. April's Life Path is 7 — the Seeker. |
| Lot of Fortune | An Arabic Part calculated from the Ascendant, Sun, and Moon. Describes where luck, flow, and peak vitality are found. April's Lot of Fortune is in Cancer H7. |
| Midheaven (MC) | The highest point in the natal chart, representing public identity, career, legacy, and what one is known for. April's Midheaven is at Libra 24°59', conjunct Spica. |
| Midpoint | The mathematical midpoint between any two planets or points in the chart. When a third planet or point falls on a midpoint, it activates the combined energy of the two planets whose midpoint it occupies. |
| Natal Chart | The map of the sky at the exact moment of birth — a snapshot of the cosmos frozen in time, encoding the energies, tendencies, gifts, and challenges that will shape the arc of the life. |
| North Node | The point in the chart representing the soul's evolutionary direction in this lifetime — where the soul is called to grow. April's North Node is at Aquarius 5°17' H1. |
| Part of Fortune | See Lot of Fortune. |
| Prenatal Solar Eclipse | The Solar Eclipse that occurred before the birth. Prenatal eclipses seed the soul's most important unfinished business from before birth. April's prenatal Solar Eclipse occurred at 29°04' Cancer H7 on July 22, 1990. |
| Progressed Chart | A chart derived by advancing the natal chart at the rate of one day per year. The progressed chart describes the slow, deep movement of the soul through time — the chart's interior clock. |
| Retrograde | A planet appears to move backward in the sky from Earth's perspective. Natal retrograde planets describe energies that are internalized, processed inward rather than outward, and often expressed with a delay or a particular depth. |
| Sabian Symbols | A system of 360 symbolic images, one for each degree of the zodiac, channeled in 1925 by clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler and astrologer Marc Edmund Jones. Each symbol acts as a picture that holds more truth than a paragraph of analysis. |
| Saturn Return | The transit that occurs when Saturn returns to its natal position — approximately every 29.5 years. The Saturn Return separates the person you were performing from the person you actually are. |
| Secondary Progressions | See Progressed Chart. |
| Sect | The ancient Hellenistic principle of day and night charts. Day charts and night charts have different sect assignments, affecting which planets are most constructive and which are most challenging. |
| Solar Arc Directions | A timing technique in which every natal planet is advanced at the same rate as the progressed Sun — approximately 1° per year. Solar Arc contacts reveal the major life themes of each year with extraordinary precision. |
| Solar Return | The chart calculated for the moment the transiting Sun returns to its exact natal degree. Provides a detailed forecast for the coming year. |
| South Node | The point in the chart representing the soul's accumulated past — the patterns, gifts, and tendencies developed across previous lifetimes. April's South Node is at Leo 5°17' H7. |
| Stellium | Three or more planets in the same sign or house. April has a triple Capricorn stellium in H1 (Saturn, Uranus, Neptune) and a triple Virgo/Leo stellium in H8 (Sun, Mercury, Venus). |
| Triseptile | An aspect of 154°17', belonging to the 7th harmonic series. Associated with fate, karma, and the mysterious arrangements that seem to transcend ordinary causality. April's Sun-Moon triseptile has an orb of 0°02' — essentially exact. |
| Vertex | A mathematical point — sometimes called the electric Ascendant — that marks the place where the ecliptic intersects the prime vertical. Planets conjunct the natal Vertex describe fated encounters and relationships. |
