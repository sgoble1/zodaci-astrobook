The journal is the instrument. What follows is a complete guide to using the journal as the primary spiritual practice.

The Daily Journal Practice: The most important journaling practice for April's chart is the daily morning journal — writing for 15-20 minutes every morning before engaging with the external world. This is the Aries Moon H3 bucket handle's daily maintenance. The writing does not need to be good. It does not need to be coherent. It does not need to be anything other than honest. The Aries Moon needs to speak first, before the Mercury Rx Virgo H8's editorial precision has had a chance to refine and manage the expression. Write what you feel. Write what you think. Write what arrived in the night. Write what you are afraid of. Write what you want. Write what you know. Write it all, without editing, without judgment, without the performance of being a good writer. The journal is not an audience. It is a mirror.

The Weekly Journaling Prompt Practice: The 52 weekly journaling prompts in Chapter 57 are designed to be used one per week over the course of a year. The weekly prompt practice is the Mercury Rx Virgo H8's most natural rhythm — the sustained, weekly engagement with the deep material that allows the volcanic mind to build pressure and release in its own time.
