# Decan Analysis

The Sub-Rulers of Your Planets

Each of the twelve signs of the zodiac is divided into three sections of
ten degrees each, called decans (from the Greek *dekanoi*, meaning "ten
each"). Each decan is governed by a planet of the same element as the
sign, adding a secondary planetary resonance to any planet occupying
that decan. The first decan of any sign carries the most direct,
unmodified expression of the sign's energy. The second decan adds a
secondary planetary color that deepens and sometimes complicates the
sign's themes. The third decan adds a tertiary planetary influence that
brings the sign to its most complex and wisdom-carrying expression — the
energy of the sign having been fully lived through. Reading decans
reveals a layer of planetary nuance that pure sign interpretation cannot
reach, explaining why two people with the same Sun sign can feel so
fundamentally different at the soul level. For April's chart, the decan
placements add extraordinary specificity to what the planets are
actually doing.

Sun — 13°56' Virgo — 2nd Decan, Ruled by Saturn

The Sun in the second decan of Virgo — the decan governed by Saturn — is
the most structurally oriented, architecturally patient, and
long-horizon focused of all Virgo Sun positions. This decan explains the
extraordinary resonance between the Virgo Sun and the Saturn Rx in H1:
these two placements are not merely in aspect. They share a deep
planetary kinship through the decan ruler. The Saturn decan of Virgo Sun
is the analyst who does not stop at analysis but moves into design — who
sees the problem with Virgoan precision and then applies Saturnian
discipline to building the solution that will actually hold. These are
the people who build things that last, who measure twice before cutting,
who are willing to work in the long time horizons that most people
cannot sustain. The shadow of the Saturn decan of Virgo Sun is the
possibility of perfectionism so deep that nothing is ever finished
enough to release — the architect who keeps refining the blueprint and
never breaks ground. The healing is trusting that the structure you have
built internally is sound enough to be tested by the external world.

Moon — 6°37' Aries — 1st Decan, Ruled by Mars

The Moon in the first decan of Aries — governed by Mars — is the most
undiluted and unapologetic Aries Moon configuration possible. Mars rules
Aries, and Mars rules this decan: the Moon here is double-Martian in its
emotional expression. This is the Moon of the warrior, the pioneer, the
one whose feelings arrive first, loudest, and with total conviction
before any more deliberate planetary energy has had time to check the
landscape. The bucket handle function of this Moon is amplified by the
Mars decan: when the entire chart's energy flows through the handle into
H3 communication and voice, it flows through a Mars-ruled Mars decan
channel — meaning the output is direct, urgent, and does not naturally
soften itself for the audience's comfort. The discipline work of the
lifetime has been learning when to honor the immediacy of this Moon and
when to allow the Saturn H1 filter to ensure that the directness serves
rather than destroys. The gift of this decan is that the emotional body
is never confused about what it wants or what it feels. The wound is
that the world sometimes punishes this clarity in someone who has been
told they are a burden for taking up space. The North Node work is
learning that the directness is a service, not a transgression.

Mercury — 16°35' Virgo Rx — 2nd Decan, Ruled by Saturn

Mercury in the same Saturn-ruled second decan of Virgo as the Sun is a
confirmation of extraordinary inner consistency: the mind and the
identity are governed by the same decan energy, which means April's
thinking and April's self-expression are not at cross-purposes. They are
co-authors. The Saturn decan of Mercury in Virgo is the analyst's
analyst: the mind that never settles for the surface understanding, that
sees not just the error but the precise nature of the error, not just
the pattern but the mechanism that generates the pattern. Retrograde
Mercury in this decan means that the most powerful version of this
analytical capacity operates in the interior — in the private thinking
space, in the journal, in the conversations with the self that happen
during the walk or in the shower or in the stillness before sleep. The
external expression of this Mercury, when it finally speaks, has the
quality of someone who has thought about this for a very long time: the
precision is not labored. It is the natural result of the internal
Saturn-Mercury process having already done every iteration of the
analysis before the word reaches the air. This is one reason her words,
when carefully chosen, tend to land with unusual force in other people.
She has already done the work that makes precision possible.

Venus — 29°20' Leo — 3rd Decan, Ruled by Mars

The third decan of Leo — governed by Mars — is the most action-oriented,
desire-driven, and will-filled decan of the fixed fire sign, and your
anaretic Venus occupies it at the very final degree before the
threshold. This is a Venus who loves with the entirety of the body as
well as the heart — who does not distinguish between love and desire,
who brings Martian directness and Leonine generosity to the same moment,
who would rather burn completely in love than protect herself from
loving. At 29°20', this Venus has been through every expression of this
decan's passionate, courageous, sometimes reckless love across multiple
lifetimes. The exhaustion at the anaretic degree is not disillusionment
— it is completion. Every lesson about what love can be when it is given
without reserve has been learned. What remains at the threshold of the
30th degree is the distillation: the knowledge that love this powerful,
love this complete, belongs in the hands of someone who can actually
hold it. The Mars decan Venus at 29° Leo is not a damaged lover. She is
a completed one, waiting for the container worthy of what she brings.

Mars — 3°02' Gemini — 1st Decan, Ruled by Jupiter

Mars in the first decan of Gemini — governed by Jupiter — is one of the
most generative and philosophically expansive creative energy signatures
in the chart. This is the decan that connects your physical drive and
creative action directly to your chart ruler: Mars's energy, in the
first decan of Gemini, is inherently Jupiterian. It expands. It finds
connection between ideas that seemed separate. It generates through
movement and conversation and the rapid-fire cross-pollination of
information streams. Jupiter ruling this Mars decan means that what you
create, when Mars energy is fully active and uninhibited, tends to be
larger in scope than what anyone initially expected — a conversation
becomes a philosophy; a project becomes a system; a task becomes a
teaching. The H5 placement adds play and joy to this configuration: the
creative fire is most fully itself when it is allowed to be genuinely
joyful, genuinely curious, genuinely multi-directional. The Process
Guide work at its best uses this first decan Gemini-Jupiter Mars energy:
systems thinking, cross-functional connection, the ability to hold
multiple tracks simultaneously and see where they intersect.

Jupiter — 3°59' Leo — 1st Decan, Ruled by the Sun

The chart ruler in the Sun-ruled first decan of Leo is the most purely
magnificent Jupiter possible — the philosopher-king energy at full
expression, the one whose expansion is genuinely regal, genuinely
generous, genuinely solar. Jupiter in the Sun decan of Leo in H7 says
that the people who expand your life, the partnerships that open
entirely new territories of possibility, the connections that change the
trajectory of what is happening — all carry a solar, Leo quality. They
are warm, creative, generous, genuinely vital. They do not expand your
life through cold efficiency or strategic utility. They expand it
through the warmth of their presence, the quality of the light they
bring, the ways in which their genuine nature makes everything around
them more alive. The Jupiter Return currently active in Leo H7 is
bringing these people. They may have already begun to appear. The
Sun-ruled Jupiter decan says: receive them with the full openness of the
Sun receiving light — not with the Leo South Node's performance mode,
but with the genuine, unguarded openness of a solar body receiving
warmth. They are not coming to orbit you. They are coming to travel
alongside you. Let them.

Saturn — 18°55' Capricorn Rx — 2nd Decan, Ruled by Venus

The Saturn in the second decan of Capricorn — governed by Venus — is one
of the most quietly beautiful decan placements in your chart, because it
reveals something that the stern exterior of Saturn H1 Rx Capricorn
might initially conceal: the architect cares about beauty. The
structures April builds are not utilitarian boxes. They are carefully
considered, aesthetically intentional, relationally warm in their
design. Venus ruling the second decan of Capricorn says that the
disciplined, patient, long-horizon quality of Saturn in domicile is
filtered through a Venusian lens — which means the discipline is not
cold, the structure is not punishing, the authority is not harsh. It is
the authority of someone who builds things that are both functional and
lovely, both enduring and humane. The Saturn Rx quality adds: the
structure is being built from the inside, through internal revision and
refinement, before it is externalized. The Venus decan says: the
interior architecture is beautiful. It is carefully, lovingly designed.
When it finally becomes fully visible to the outside world — in the
leadership role, in the process systems she builds, in the way she leads
people — those who see it will recognize something that is both
impressive in its rigor and genuinely beautiful in its care for the
humans inside it.
