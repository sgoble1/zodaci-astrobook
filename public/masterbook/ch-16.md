*Where Luck Flows*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

The Part of Fortune in Cancer in the seventh house is unambiguous: your luck, flow, and peak vitality are found through the themes of Cancer (emotional depth, nurturing, belonging, the mother-principle) expressed in the house of partnership (H7). You are luckiest when you are emotionally authentic in relationship — when you let yourself be seen and held.

Fortune here also suggests your natural luck increases near water, near home environments, near anything that triggers the feeling of safe belonging. When you are in a relationship that truly matches your capacity for depth and devotion, every other area of your life tends to expand simultaneously. Love is not a side note in your chart; it is the chart ruler's home.

The Part of Fortune in Cancer H7 sitting near Chiron in Cancer H7 creates a profound and poignant configuration: your greatest luck and your deepest wound occupy the same territory. This is not cruel design — it is the chart's most elegant teaching. The wound of belonging, when healed, becomes the source of your greatest fortune. The place where you were most hurt is the place where your abundance lives. The healing of Chiron Cancer H7 is not just emotional work; it is the activation of your Part of Fortune.
