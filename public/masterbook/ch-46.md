The 7 is the number of the philosopher, the researcher, the one who needs solitude to think clearly and who finds the noise of the social world exhausting rather than energizing. It is the number of the person who reads everything, who questions everything, who cannot accept a surface answer when a deeper one is available. It is the number of the person who knows things that others don't know how to know.
