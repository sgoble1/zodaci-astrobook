Section 29: Nutritional Astrology —

Feeding the Chart Nutritional astrology — the tradition of aligning dietary and supplemental choices with planetary and sign rulerships — is one of the oldest branches of medical astrology, with roots in Hippocratic medicine and the Galenic tradition of the ancient world. The principle is straightforward: each zodiac sign rules specific organs, systems, and tissues of the physical body, and each planet governs specific physiological processes. When a natal chart shows particular placements in specific signs and houses, the associated body systems are either constitutionally strong (if the planet is in domicile or exaltation) or constitutionally sensitive (if the planet is in detriment or fall, retrograde, or heavily aspected). For April, the natal chart produces a specific map of constitutional strengths and vulnerabilities that, when translated into nutritional guidance, creates a highly personalized dietary framework that supports the body the chart was designed to inhabit. The dominant sign energy in April's chart is Capricorn, with Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune all concentrated in the first house in this sign. Capricorn rules the skeletal system — specifically the knees, joints, teeth, and skin — and its three- planet stellium in the first house makes these areas constitutionally significant.
