### SPICA ON THE MIDHEAVEN — 'The Luckiest Star in Astrology'

Spica is the brightest star in the constellation Virgo — the alpha star, the one that gives the constellation its name and its identity. In the ancient world, Spica was one of the most important navigational stars, used by sailors to find their way across the open ocean. It is associated with the wheat sheaf that Virgo holds in her hand — the harvest of extraordinary effort, the reward that comes to those who have done the genuine work of cultivation. Spica is the star of the harvest.

Spica conjunct your Midheaven at Libra 24°59' — just 51 minutes of arc from the exact conjunction — is the most benefic fixed star contact possible for career. The luckiest star in astrology sits on your public face. You were born with starlight on your résumé. This does not mean effortless — it means that the effort you put into your genuine life's work will be recognized and rewarded in ways that feel, to you, almost disproportionate. The harvest will come. Trust the growing season.

Spica's blessing is particularly strong for writers, healers, teachers, and those who work with invisible truths — the people who cultivate something that cannot be immediately seen or measured, whose work requires the patience of the farmer who plants in spring and harvests in autumn. This is the quality of your career: the long cultivation, the patient tending, the trust that the harvest will come even when the growing season feels endless.

The meditation for this star: sit quietly and feel the starlight on your résumé. What is the genuine work of cultivation that you have been doing — the work that cannot be immediately seen or measured, that requires the patience of the farmer? What is the harvest that is coming? What is the recognition that Spica promises — not the recognition that comes from performing, but the recognition that comes from the genuine work of cultivation? Trust the growing season. The harvest will come.
