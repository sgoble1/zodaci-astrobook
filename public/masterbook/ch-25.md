The dispositor chain is one of the most elegant and revealing techniques in traditional astrology. Every planet is 'disposed of' by the ruler of the sign it occupies — meaning that the planet's energy is ultimately governed by, and must express itself through, the planet that rules its sign. When you follow these chains of rulership from every planet in the chart, they eventually converge on a single planet: the final dispositor. In your chart, that planet is Mercury.
