Neptune in Capricorn in the first house, retrograde, is one of the most significant placements in April's chart for understanding her relationship with the unseen. Neptune rules dissolution — the thinning of boundaries, the permeability of the membrane between visible and invisible, the capacity to receive what cannot be seen with ordinary eyes. In the first house, this energy is not somewhere across the room; it is woven into the structure of April's embodied experience of moving through the world. She has always been permeable to unseen presences. The moments of feeling accompanied when she is technically alone, the sense of being watched over during the hardest hours, the inexplicable knowing that arrives just before a significant moment — these are not imagination. They are Neptune H1 receiving what is there to be received. She is, by chart design, a receiver. The retrograde quality of Neptune turns the function inward, making it more internal and personal and less externally visible — which means the communication comes primarily through internal channels: feeling, dream, sudden knowing, the sensation of warmth or presence that has no physical origin.
