# Tarot Birth Cards & Monthly Oracle Guidance

*Your Tarot birth cards are calculated from your birth date: 9 + 6 + 1 +
9 + 9 + 0 = 34; 3 + 4 = 7. The primary card is **The Chariot (VII)**.
The secondary card is found by adding the digits of the primary: The
Tower (XVI, from 7 + 9 = 16). Together, they form an inseparable pair —
the vehicle of will and the necessary deconstruction that frees the will
to move in the right direction. The hidden card that follows The Tower
is **The Star (XVII)** — and this is the energy of 2026–2027.*

Monthly Oracle Guidance

|                                           |
|-------------------------------------------|
| January — The Chariot Steers by Starlight |

January asks you to set the year's intention from will rather than from
reaction. The Chariot energy: what do you want to have accomplished by
December? Name it now, with Virgo precision (Mercury Rx) — not a vague
wish, but a specific direction. The Star energy: trust that what you
pour out will be replenished. Your word for January: *Direction.*

|                                   |
|-----------------------------------|
| February — The Star Fills the Cup |

February under The Star's frequency is a month of receiving — of
allowing the renewal that January's intention has set into motion. For a
chart that gives so readily, February asks specifically: what are you
receiving? What assistance, what grace, what unexpected gift has
arrived? Your resistance to receiving is February's work. Your word for
February: *Receive.*

|                                         |
|-----------------------------------------|
| March — The Tower Examines What Remains |

March carries The Tower energy in a quieter key — not collapse, but
honest audit. What in your life is still built on the wrong foundation?
Not to tear it down dramatically, but to acknowledge it with the
precision of Mercury Rx and the honesty of Pluto H10. What is no longer
true? What are you maintaining out of habit rather than love? Your word
for March: *Honest.*

|                                  |
|----------------------------------|
| April — The Birthday of Becoming |

April is your name month — and in 2026, with the North Node activations
and the Jupiter Return underway, your personal April carries an electric
quality. The Chariot and The Star combine this month into a single
frequency: the victor who dares to hope. Whatever threshold you have
been standing in front of, this month invites one step through. Your
word for April: *Threshold.*

|                                |
|--------------------------------|
| May — The Star's Middle Waters |

May is a month of sustained trust — not the dramatic trust of
threshold-crossing, but the quieter trust of continuing. The Star figure
does not pour water once; she pours it continuously, in perfect
equilibrium, trusting the replenishment. May asks you to sustain the
flow you established in April without testing it with doubt. Your word
for May: *Sustain.*

|                                  |
|----------------------------------|
| June — The Chariot at Full Speed |

June brings the Chariot's momentum — this is when the sphinxes are
finally, fully aligned and the chariot moves. With Uranus having just
crossed natal Mars (exact in late June 2026 as this compendium is
written), the creative drive has been electrified. June is the month to
let it run. Do not rein it in. Your word for June: *Release.*

|                                  |
|----------------------------------|

July invites a review of what the Tower cleared and what that clearing
made possible. Not grief for the rubble, but gratitude for the space.
What are you now building in the space that the Tower created? Jupiter
in Leo is expanding your H7 — partnerships, visibility, the chart
ruler's homecoming. July is spacious, golden, expansive. Your word for
July: *Space.*

*Release the Stage.*

December is the Star at her most luminous — the year's work completed,

the replenishment. Let the year's gifts settle into the body before the
new year asks you to move again. Your word for December: *Pour.*
