Every house in the natal chart is governed by a planet — the ruler of the sign on the house cusp. The house ruler describes the planet that governs the themes of that house, and its placement in the chart describes how those themes are expressed and where they find their most natural outlet. The Jupiter H7 ruler of H1 also means that her greatest blessings arrive through people — that the expansion of her identity happens through the mirror of the significant other.
