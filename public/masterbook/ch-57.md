The following 52 journaling prompts are organized by the major themes of your natal chart. They are designed to be used one per week over the course of a year, though you may return to any prompt at any time. The prompts are not questions to be answered correctly — they are invitations to the Aries Moon H3 bucket handle to open, to the Mercury Rx Virgo H8 volcanic mind to build pressure and release, to the authentic self to speak without management or apology.
