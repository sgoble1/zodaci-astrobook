*Love, Beauty, and the Anaretic Degree*

✦ ◆ ★ ◆ ✦

Venus retrograde occurs approximately every 18 months, lasting about 40 days each time. For you, with Venus at the anaretic 29° Leo in the 8th house conjunct the Vertex, Venus retrograde periods carry a particular intensity — they are periods when the themes of love, beauty, self-worth, and fated encounter are reviewed, reconsidered, and often dramatically activated.

### Venus Retrograde in Leo: The Most Personal

When Venus turns retrograde in Leo — which occurs approximately every 8 years — it passes directly over your natal Venus at 29°20' Leo. This is the most personally significant Venus retrograde possible: the planet of love and beauty returning to its natal position, reviewing the entire history of your relationship with love, beauty, and self-worth. Venus retrograde in Leo asks: have you been loving yourself with the same completeness you offer others? Have you been receiving love with the same openness you give it? Have you been honoring the regal quality of your Venus — the Regulus conjunction, the anaretic degree — or have you been settling for less than what the 29th degree of Leo has earned the right to expect?

### Venus Retrograde in Aries: The Bucket Handle Activated

When Venus turns retrograde in Aries — which occurs approximately every 8 years — it activates the territory of your natal Moon H3, the bucket handle. Venus retrograde in Aries asks: are you expressing love through your communication? Are you allowing your emotional directness (Aries Moon) to be an expression of love rather than a liability? Are you communicating your needs in relationship with the same courage your Aries Moon is capable of? This Venus retrograde period often produces important realizations about the relationship between emotional expression and love.
