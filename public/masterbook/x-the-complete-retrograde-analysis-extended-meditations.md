Every planet in the solar system turns retrograde periodically — appearing to move backward in the sky from Earth's perspective as the relative orbital speeds of Earth and the planet create the optical illusion of reverse motion. Retrograde periods are not times of breakdown or failure; they are times of review, revision, and the deepening of the themes associated with the retrograde planet. For April, with four natal retrograde planets (Mercury, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune), retrograde seasons carry a particular significance — they are periods when the chart's most important energies return to their natural inward-processing mode.
