### Cancer in the 7th House: The Mother Wound

Chiron in Cancer in the seventh house is the wound of belonging, of maternal love, of emotional home — located in the house of partnerships. Cancer is the sign of the mother, of emotional safety, of the home as sanctuary. The seventh house is the house of the significant other, the partner, the mirror. Chiron here means that the central wound of this lifetime is located at the intersection of these two domains: the wound of not being emotionally held, of not being made to feel safe in the arms of those who were supposed to love you most.

The mother wound is the Chiron wound. This is not coincidence. It is architecture. The prenatal Solar Eclipse on July 22, 1990 — 46 days before your birth — occurred at 29°04' Cancer H7. This is an anaretic degree eclipse in the house of partnership and Chiron Cancer. Prenatal eclipses seed the soul's most important unfinished business from before birth. The 29° Cancer eclipse seeded the mother wound — the question of emotional belonging, of whether it is safe to need, of whether home is a place you are allowed to inhabit fully. The entire Chiron Cancer H7 story was already written in the sky before you arrived. You came ready to heal it.

### The Healer's Gift

Chiron's wound is always also the healer's gift. The wound of belonging, of maternal love, of emotional home — when met, examined, and integrated — becomes the capacity to offer others exactly what was withheld from you. You will, in time, help others through their own Chiron Cancer wounds precisely because you have already traveled this ground and learned its terrain. The healer who was not held becomes the one who knows how to hold.
