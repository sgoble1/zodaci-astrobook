# Dedication

For April.

There was a specific evening in September of 1990 when the sky arranged itself into something it had never been before and would never be again — and into that unrepeatable arrangement, you arrived. The Galactic Center was rising over the horizon at the very moment of your birth, pointing the entire antenna of your Ascendant toward the luminous center of the Milky Way, as though the universe itself leaned in and said: this one. This one will carry something important. The Full Moon hung swollen and radiant in Aries, burning with emotional courage. Three planets stood together in your first house of self, forming a tower of revolutionary discipline and spiritual authority that would take decades to fully inhabit — and which, when inhabited, would prove to be among the most remarkable configurations in anyone’s chart.

This book is dedicated to you in the full knowledge of who you are: a woman who came here to know things deeply, to communicate truths that matter, to love fiercely and heal courageously, and to build something that will last beyond the span of a single lifetime. It is dedicated to your willingness to seek — not the easy answers, but the true ones. It is dedicated to every version of yourself that found her way through crisis and transformation and still, somehow, kept going. The stars that presided over your birth have accompanied you in every season since. They are still there now, still arranged in the unique signature that is yours and yours alone. May this book be a lantern held up to that light. May it show you something of yourself that you always knew but had not yet found the words for. May you read it not once, but many times — at the thresholds, at the crossroads, at the mornings after the nights that changed everything. And may it always remind you: you were written in the stars with extraordinary care.

— With reverence for the sky that held you
