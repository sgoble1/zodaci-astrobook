# AstroBook mobile and workbook upgrade

## What will change

- Rebrand the private experience as **AstroBook**: replace “Companion Document,” update the mobile/desktop brand treatment, and create a compact purple-and-yellow star logo beside the name.
- Change the main-page **Begin the Report** action to **Explore**, linking directly to Explore & Discover.
- Repair the phone experience so the brand bar, menu button, full navigation, page content, forms, charts, tables, and bottom audio controls remain visible, readable, and easy to operate.
- Add richer visual tools to each workbook chapter without removing its current explanations or questions. Each visual will translate that chapter’s idea into something concrete, such as a two-sided balance, thought loop, trigger/body map, trust rings, needs comparison, responsibility load, belonging compass, purpose map, or cycle view.
- Keep every visual understandable in ordinary language, keyboard accessible, screen-reader described, and usable without relying on color alone.

## Mobile behavior

- Use a clear AstroBook top bar with a reliable full-screen menu drawer, visible close control, scrollable sections, large touch targets, safe-area spacing, and focus management.
- Prevent narrow-screen clipping in headings, navigation labels, workbook controls, charts, tables, dialogs, and long text.
- Preserve the current desktop sidebar while sharing the same menu source between desktop, mobile, and Instructions.

## Logo direction

- Create two polished logo concepts using violet and warm yellow stars, inspect both, and use the clearest compact version beside the exact text **AstroBook**.
- Keep the logo readable at small phone-header size and compatible with the existing dark cosmic interface.

## Verification

- Test the home page, Explore & Discover, workbook overview, every workbook chapter, and representative long/form/chart pages at phone and desktop sizes.
- Verify opening, scrolling, navigating, closing, keyboard trapping/return focus, screen-reader names, no overlaps, and no blank states.
- Confirm the app builds cleanly and the current private-chat session handling remains intact.
