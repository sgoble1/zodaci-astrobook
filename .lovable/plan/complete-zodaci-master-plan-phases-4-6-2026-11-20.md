3. **Gift claim:** extend paid gift orders with a secure claim link. Require sign-in and verified recipient identity; store only hashed, expiring, single-use tokens and claim atomically. Recipient birth facts and shipping address belong to the gift, never overwrite the buyer's or recipient's profile. Generate the instant reader from the gift recipient's facts, with unknown-time handling, then freeze the reviewed edition through the existing paid fulfillment process. Handle expired, revoked, already-claimed, unpaid, and failed-save states.
4. **Public ephemeris:** add `/ephemeris` through canonical public navigation. Compute one selected month at a time, 1800–2100, at 00:00 UTC; filter planet and retrograde status, navigate dates, and expose validated historical event lookups from independent reference fixtures. No substitute/mock positions on errors and no unsupported Chiron coverage claims.

## Technical boundaries
- Use React/Vite, existing semantic theme tokens and shadcn controls; no heavy libraries in the main bundle.
- Calculation/content helpers remain pure and independently tested; page code only presents results.
- New gift/sync application records require explicit grants, RLS and server-enforced ownership. Reuse existing tables where their contracts fit; inspect the live schema before migrations.
- Secrets remain server-side. Existing Lulu keys are present; never re-request them without a failed connection signal. Shopify app credentials/signing setup are a real external prerequisite, not replaced with connector-managed tokens.
- No founder archive payload in public source. Shared readers are identity-fenced and clear on account change.
- Record technical decisions within the existing instruction-file budget; roadmap.md remains the sole work index. Phases 1–3 and unrelated findings are out of scope except required consumers/regressions.
