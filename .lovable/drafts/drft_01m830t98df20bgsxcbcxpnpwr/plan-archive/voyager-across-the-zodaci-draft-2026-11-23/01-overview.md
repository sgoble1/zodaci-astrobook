# Voyager across the Zodaci draft

Extend the new home-screen direction into the rest of this draft: warm paper, dark engraved ink, restrained brass and vintage red, celestial cutout artwork, and dimensional display lettering. Keep Zodaci’s identity and familiar destinations.

## What changes

1. **Shared foundations and navigation:** carry the new palette into public and member page surroundings, menus, forms, dialogs and footers. Make the active destination clear. Keep the four public pillars and member deck groups rather than adding competing navigation.
2. **Public experience:** restyle `/welcome`, About, pricing, Learn, calculators, ephemeris, relationships, product pages and the demo. Replace the busiest combinations of competing panels with a clear page opening, primary action, and grouped supporting detail. Preserve useful content and links.
3. **Member experience:** apply the same editorial hierarchy to dashboards, chart details, readings, journals, audio and shared spaces. Keep personal charts and authorized founder editions connected to their existing data. Make the dashboard a place to resume rather than a wall of equal-weight options.
4. **Artwork and motion:** reuse the new engraved artwork, retain relevant painted waitlist/observatory imagery, and create a small coordinated set of supporting illustrations where needed. Represent different races naturally. Use celestial portals at intentional openings, not behind dense forms or every card. Slow decorative motion and reduced-motion support remain.
5. **Product and specialist surfaces:** refresh the surroundings of AstroBook, AstroComic, shop, investor and admin pages. Preserve book jackets, interior plates, comic stories, product photographs, chart geometry, animated TVs and cassette controls; avoid flattening them into one generic paper surface.

## Scope protections

- This work changes this draft, not the published site.
- No changes to prices, entitlements, checkout, calculations, private-content permissions or email delivery.
- Existing slides and valuable content remain available; the default arrangement becomes calmer.
- Account-specific or paid views must be checked with authorized access, never bypassed.
