# SEO Review

## Gather actual signals

Use available SEO findings, Search Console diagnostics and current production HTTP/rendered-page checks. Use Semrush for relevant domain, keyword and competitor evidence when available. Report unavailable integrations as missing evidence; an empty third-party report does not prove no search demand.

Record domain, market, device, date and data source. Do not call research the latest without checking its date. Keep production observations distinct from preview-only changes.

## Review in dependency order

1. **Indexing boundaries:** inspect status codes, robots directives, sitemap and canonical URLs for intended public pages. Keep account, admin, chat, private-edition and personal chart content out of search. Robots rules and noindex are not access controls.
2. **Delivery:** check whether meaningful public content and metadata reach crawlers; client-side source presence alone is insufficient evidence. Check broken links, redirect chains, missing pages and mobile usability.
3. **Page semantics:** assess unique titles/descriptions, heading hierarchy, descriptive links, appropriate image alternatives and matching social metadata. Social previews are separate from search ranking.
4. **Structured data:** use only types supported by truthful visible content. Validate eligibility; never add fabricated reviews, ratings or offers. Structured data does not guarantee rich results.
5. **Search intent and architecture:** map public offers and editorial pages to researched queries, avoid cannibalization and thin repetitive AI pages, and improve useful internal links.
6. **Performance:** measure available loading, interaction and layout-shift signals. Distinguish lab results from field Core Web Vitals and do not invent scores.

## Report and verify

Separate confirmed failures, opportunities and measurement gaps. For each failure provide affected URL, evidence, severity, exact remedy and recheck. Recheck fixed behavior, not merely source edits; clearly mark production changes awaiting publication or reindexing. Avoid mass-generated location/keyword pages or publishing private personalized readings as an SEO shortcut.
