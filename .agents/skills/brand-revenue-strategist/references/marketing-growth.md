# Marketing & Revenue Growth

## Audit the current offer

Inventory real audiences, problems, free/paid boundaries, prices, differentiators, product previews, purchase friction and delivery promises. Check whether claims match actual working capabilities. Compare alternatives using current dated research; use marketing-research and Semrush tools when available rather than unsupported competitor assumptions.

Map discovery → account → saved birth details → first useful reading → upgrade intent → verified payment → use/download/fulfillment → return. Adapt to the app's actual steps. Identify missing evidence or events before asserting funnel conversion.

Define numerator, denominator, time window and exclusions for metrics. Aggregate upgrade clicks and payments are not an ordered user cohort. Exclude test payments and distinguish gross signups from activated customers; use authorized aggregate records without exposing individuals.

## Recommend focused experiments

Prioritize a small set of channels that fit audience intent and verified delivery capacity: useful public editorial, product samples, organic social, consent-based lifecycle email, partnerships or tightly scoped paid acquisition. Do not propose acquiring more customers into broken checkout or unfulfillable book orders.

For each experiment specify:
- Audience and customer problem; substantiated offer and message.
- Channel, landing destination and creative concept tied to the actual product.
- Hypothesis, baseline, primary outcome and attribution window.
- Smallest deliverable, effort, proposed budget cap and approval needed.
- Contribution-margin guardrail, success criterion and stop condition.

Treat numerical budgets or conversion targets without historical data as proposed scenarios, not forecasts. Coordinate with the profitability reference before recommending discounts, free generation or paid ad scale. Prefer value-driven retention over generating all unused content upfront.

## Trust and constraints

No invented social proof, fake urgency, hidden fees, guaranteed outcomes or misleading astrology certainty. Keep marketing distinct from counseling and private user content. Do not send emails without consent and configured sender infrastructure, spend ad budget, change offers or publish campaigns without authorization. Verify the linked customer journey before describing a campaign as launch-ready.
