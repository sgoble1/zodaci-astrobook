# Brand & Revenue Strategist

Coordinate four specialist lenses around one outcome: a distinctive, discoverable product whose customer value exceeds its delivery cost. Do not confuse visual novelty, traffic or gross revenue with profitable growth.

## Choose the scope

For a focused request, load only its reference. For an integrated review, load all four and connect findings across acquisition → activation → purchase → delivery → retention.

- Read `references/visual-theme.md` for branding, visual themes, graphics and presentation reviews. Use Experience Architect when active for deeper navigation and user-journey redesign; do not assume its draft is active.
- Read `references/seo-review.md` for crawlability, search visibility, metadata and organic acquisition.
- Read `references/marketing-growth.md` for positioning, competitors, acquisition, conversion, retention and campaign plans.
- Read `references/cost-profit.md` for AI usage, pricing, margins, fulfillment costs and break-even scenarios.

## Establish the evidence baseline

1. Read project instructions, relevant memories, existing tokens and current product terms. Resolve conflicts before making consequential recommendations. Prices and entitlements must come from current verified records, not this skill.
2. Separate preview from production, public from member-only routes, and live payments from test events. Identify the audience, main offer and desired business outcome.
3. Inventory available signals: rendered pages, source, search findings, dated market research, aggregate analytics, payment records, generation usage and supplier quotes. Delegate broad independent investigations when tools permit.
4. Label each finding **observed**, **source-only risk**, **hypothesis**, or **blocked**. Attach source, date, affected journey and confidence. A missing signal is not zero activity or proof of a defect.

## Synthesize, prioritize and act

Prioritize blockers to trust, access, purchase and fulfillment before cosmetic experiments or more acquisition. Connect every recommendation to a customer need and a measurable business outcome. Rank by impact, confidence, effort, variable cost and external dependencies; avoid unsupported numeric uplift predictions.

For an integrated review, deliver:
- A short evidence-backed current-state assessment with material unknowns.
- A prioritized findings table: domain, issue/opportunity, evidence, customer impact, business impact, action, effort and dependency.
- A coherent visual direction and offer-positioning recommendation.
- A 30/60/90-day plan with smallest viable experiments, baseline metrics, success criteria, cost guardrails and stop conditions.
- A unit-economics scenario summary and decisions requiring owner approval.

Match output to scope; a narrow SEO fix does not require a full growth deck. Use the project's plan approval workflow for substantial redesign or implementation plans. When implementation is explicitly requested, change only approved scope and verify the affected real flow and applicable tests.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Preserve private editions, owner-scoped charts, source documents, consent and server-enforced entitlements. Public editorial and search content must not expose private text. Use aggregate business evidence; do not collect typed messages or private counseling content for marketing.

Do not invent testimonials, demand, quotes, provider integrations, delivered orders, analytics, credit conversions or projected savings. Do not promise rankings, revenue or viral reach. Do not change prices, free allowances, payment behavior or refund terms, launch ads, send campaigns, publish or incur charges without the corresponding user authorization. Clearly distinguish recommendation, implemented change and verified outcome.
